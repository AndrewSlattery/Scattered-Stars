# The Polletio

A mid-range freighter of the Arrhenos Merchant Fleet.

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Crew | 2,800 |
| Route | Hesperine Circuit |
| Age | 97 years (with refits every decade) |

## The Hesperine Circuit

Arrhenos → Tethys-Var → three Mandate waypoints → Korsen's Anchorage (Drift hub) → home via Vega trade stations, with dozens of stops at minor ports and rec stations in between.

A full circuit takes fourteen months.

## Ship's Gender

Arrhene ships are gendered masculine—a linguistic quirk carried over from the generation ships. The Polletio *himself*, then.

Crew talk about him as though he has opinions:

- "He's running hot" = environmental systems are off
- "He doesn't like that cargo" = weight distribution feels wrong

## Daily Routine

A standard day runs two half-shifts of three hours each, separated by at least six hours.

### Departments

**Engineering** divides into:

- Drive (AV systems)
- Power (fusion reactors, distribution)
- Structure (bulkheads, floors, hull)
- Systems (everything else)

**Navigation** keeps its own hours, clustered around jump calculations. Their mess has better coffee—everyone knows this, nobody knows why.

## Living Spaces

### Bunks

Ordinary crew sleep in compartments of eighteen—three three-high stacks of cots, two men per cot, barely a metre wide.

You learn your bunkmate's breathing, his smell, whether he kicks in dreams. Some men request transfers after a week. Others pair up for years, and the housing officer lets preferences sort themselves out.

Senior crew get one man per cot; officers have actual cabins.

### The Wetrooms

Sprawl across Deck Four:

- Sixty showers
- Main pool (runs cold near the filtration intake)
- Two soaking pools (properly hot)
- Steam rooms
- Sauna
- Private chambers with doors but no locks (etiquette: double knock, then wait)

There's a social geography—the port-side pool tends toward older crew, the steam room toward Drive engineers. Learning the map takes a few weeks.

### Mess

**Mess Hall One** seats two hundred, feeding the crew in rolling waves. The food is real—hydroponics, protein vats, actual cooking. Quality varies with the cook; fresh supplies from a station stop transform the menu for weeks.

Other messes range from smaller copies of One to a few seats and a stationary food-truck style setup.

### Bars

- **The Canteen** (official)
- **The Hollow** (repurposed storage bay, a reputation)
- A third with no name that moves

Engineering Lower runs a semi-authorised still with even stronger booze than the bars.

## Port Calls

The circuit puts the Polletio in port roughly every six weeks:

| Port | Character |
|------|-----------|
| Tethys-Var | Three weeks loading fez. Half the crew on enforced downtime. Rough stations where fights are common. |
| Korsen's Anchorage | Medical services, entertainment, reputation for discretion |
| Vega stations | Expensive. Men blow their savings on luxuries they'll regret. |
| Arrhenos | Chance to go home—or to stay aboard if you'd rather. About a third choose the ship. |

## Character

The Polletio isn't famous. He's not the largest ship in the Fleet, not the fastest, not on the most glamorous route.

Some men serve a contract and leave. Others stay for decades, carving out corners that become theirs.

There's a word in Hesperine dialect, **vethallen**, roughly translating as "the place that holds you"—neither home nor trap exactly, but the space you return to because returning has become what you do.

Whether that describes the Polletio depends on who you ask. But he keeps flying, circuit after circuit, and the men keep signing on.

## Crew

### Caden Orroway

**Role:** Chief Engineer  
**Age:** 54 (looks 40)  
**Service:** 31 years on the Polletio

Has turned down shore promotions twice. His reasoning is private.

The engineering sections run smoothly under him—not through force of personality but through systems built over decades: training programmes, redundancies, a culture of competence.

He eats alone more often than not. Men respect him without quite liking him, which seems to be the arrangement he prefers.

### Fenno Blackwell

**Role:** Environmental systems maintenance (official); bartender at the Hollow (actual)  
**Age:** 26

Slight, with an easy manner that makes men talk. What they tell him stays with him—a reputation cultivated carefully. The Hollow runs on his discretion as much as its smuggled spirits.

He sends money home to his fathers in Landwick every pay cycle, enough that they've stopped asking when he's coming back.

### Seoras Thain & Vel Dorian

**Seoras Thain**  
**Role:** Cargo foreman  
**Age:** 40

**Vel Dorian**  
**Role:** Communications (formerly Navigation)

They've been together for nine years. Met on a previous vessel—Vel transferred to the Polletio with Seoras when he moved up to foreman.

They share a senior double-bunk modified with a curtain for the illusion of privacy.

The relationship is romantic in a quiet, settled way. They don't perform it, don't hide it, don't explain it. Seoras makes Vel coffee before his shift; Vel waits for Seoras after his.

When they argue—about money, about family visits, about whether to renew contracts together—they do it in low voices, then one of them goes to the wetrooms until the edge wears off.

They've talked about leaving the merchant life, finding work planetside. The conversations never go anywhere. The Polletio is what they know.
