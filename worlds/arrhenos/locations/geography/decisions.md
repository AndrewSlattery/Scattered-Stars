# Arrhenos geography: decisions

Working record for the map rebuild. Letters refer to [old-layout-reference.png](old-layout-reference.png).

## Method

Top-down: position and outline of each landmass first, settled by questionnaire. Relief, drainage and climate come later. A fine grid (H3 or similar) comes last, for detail only.

Radius about Earth's; gravity lower. 20-hour day. The calendar year starts at the southern spring equinox. Seven spaceport cities lie along the equator, so land must reach the equator in at least seven places (or fewer, with more than one port on some).

## Round 1: overall

- **Layout:** same bones, redrawn. Keep the big landmasses roughly where they are; coastlines, proportions and connections are free.
- **Land share:** about 30%, like Earth.
- **Landmass B:** split B·nw off with a strait or small ocean somewhere between 150°W and 120°W, making two landmasses.
- **Questionnaire scope:** position and outline only, for now.

## Round 2: B split and A

- **B split:** at about 135°W. B·nw becomes a continent of about 17M km² (South America's size); the polar B keeps about 40M km² (Asia's size), including the Nemora and Zespol.
- **Gap between B and B·nw:** a sea a few hundred kilometres wide, like the Red Sea or Baltic. Two landmasses, but close neighbours.
- **A:** stays one long continent, about 14,000 km east to west, with the capital near the middle.
- **Campottonì's gulf:** kept. A long, sheltered sea-arm opening west, with a northern arm of land enclosing it.

## Round 3: A's edges

- **A's northern horn** (15–25°E): kept, reaching the equator. Its tip is a spaceport site, the capital's nearest.
- **A–C gap near 0°:** open sea, 1,000–1,500 km. A wide, busy crossing to Port Magnus.
- **Far south:** the mountainous peninsula below the Hesperine Plateau stays, running to about 70°S. The south pole is open ocean.
- **F:** kept as an island of about 0.9M km² off A's east end at 107°E, crossing the equator. Another spaceport candidate.

## Round 4: the Nemora, D and C

- **Nemora across the D–B gap:** a narrow strait stays between D and polar B at about 39°N. The preserve spans it, so the Nemora includes marine zones as well, which widens its range of environments. *Follow-up for canon:* [nemora-zespol.md](../nemora-zespol.md) calls it "a continuous corridor of preserved ecological zones"; that still holds, but it could now mention the strait and the undersea reserves.
- **D:** broad, with the Nemora running down its middle and settled tropical lands on both sides. The treaty line is a border within D.
- **C:** keeps about 21M km², spanning tropics to subarctic, with a southern lobe down to Port Magnus.
- **C's western inlet:** wanted in principle, but it must not look like a repeat of A's gulf. See round 5.

## Round 5: C's inlet and polar B

- **C's inlet:** a branching inland sea behind a narrow mouth on the west coast, dividing into several arms and basins like the Baltic. This distinguishes it from A's long, straight gulf.
- **B·ne:** stays attached. A great southern arm of polar B, from the ice down into temperate latitudes.
- **North pole:** land, under an ice sheet. The Nemora runs from tropical forest across the D–B strait to the polar ice sheet.
- **I:** kept, a single temperate island of about 0.2M km² off B·ne's west coast.

## Round 6: B·nw, E and the small islands

- **B·nw's south:** a long, narrow, Central-America-like peninsula with Verenstad at its equatorial tip.
- **B·nw–E:** open water, a short crossing of 500–600 km. E stays a separate continent.
- **E:** roughly as is, about 11M km², but its deep western bay needs reshaping so it does not repeat A's gulf or C's inland sea. See round 7.
- **G:** kept, a cool-temperate island of about 0.7M km² between E and A's west end.
- **H:** kept, an equatorial pair of islands around 144°W. A spaceport candidate.

## Round 7: E's coast and the spaceports

- **E's west coast:** a fjord coast in place of the single bay. It is high, wet and backed by mountains, cut by many deep fjords and sounds, and faces the prevailing westerlies at 30–50°S.
- **The seven equatorial spaceports:** A's horn, Port Magnus (C), F, H, Verenstad (B·nw), and two on D, one on each side of the Nemora.
