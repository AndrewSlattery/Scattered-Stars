# Landwick

**Coordinates:** 13°S, 72°E

Coastal cultural centre and artistic heart of Arrhenos.

## Overview

Landwick sprawls along the coast where land gives way to brackish marshland and then the sea. The climate hits immediately—Singapore meeting Mediterranean, hot and humid, with sudden rain that steams off within an hour.

## Character

The city is known for:

- Diverse architectural styles accumulated over centuries
- Hundreds of theatres and museums
- Art districts in various media
- Publishing houses
- A reputation for creative and intellectual ferment

Where Campottonì represents power and Brovdingonai represents comfortable prosperity, Landwick represents culture—both high and low, traditional and experimental.
