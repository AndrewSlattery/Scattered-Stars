# Tethys-Var

A moon of a gas giant in the Lophore system, and the most important source of fez in human space.

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Moon |
| System | Lophore |
| Primary | Gas giant |
| Significance | ~13.5% of galactic fez supply |
## Fez Production

Tethys-Var is the single largest source of [metastable iron-zinc-uranium tennessite](../setting/technology/fez.md), providing approximately 13.5% of the annual harvest.
### The Tethys Consortium

Controls 67% of harvesting licences on the moon. The Consortium's dominance makes it a major player in fez markets, though it faces competition from:

- Sable Cartel proxies
- Arrhenos Merchant Fleet traders
- Independent (often illegal) harvesters
## Population

The permanent population is modest, but millions of temporary workers arrive during unpredictable fez harvests. This transient workforce creates a boom-town atmosphere during harvest seasons.
## Infrastructure

- Dozens of orbital stations
- Surface harvesting facilities
- Processing plants
- Worker housing (temporary and permanent)
## Territorial Disputes

Multiple parties contest control over harvesting rights, processing facilities, and orbital infrastructure. These disputes occasionally escalate to violence.
## Strategic Importance

Control of Tethys-Var means influence over fez markets. Major powers and corporations vie for position through proxies, legal manoeuvring, and occasional direct action.
