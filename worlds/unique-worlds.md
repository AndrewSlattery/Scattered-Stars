# Unique Worlds

Worlds whose physical conditions shape their societies in unusual ways.

## Awyr

In the Aether system. A low-gravity world with a dense atmosphere, where simple wing-suits or even less allow humans sustained flight.

This shapes Awyr's architecture, transportation, customs and sense of space—differently in each of its societies. Flight means one thing to a fishing archipelago, another to a mountain city, and a third to the lowland communities that, to everyone else's bafflement, mostly walk.

## Periapt

In the Ptica system. A world whose orbit is elliptical enough that society transforms completely twice a year.

The variation between close approach to the star and distant retreat requires entirely different social, economic and physical arrangements for summer and winter. Periapt's people maintain two parallel civilisations and switch between them as the orbit demands.
