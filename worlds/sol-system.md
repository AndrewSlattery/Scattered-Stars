# The Sol System

Humanity's origin system, now a complex mix of pilgrimage site, industrial hub, and radical experiment.
## Earth

Governed by the United Nations—one of the few surviving pre-Scattering political structures.
Earth serves as a destination for many types of pilgrimages:

- Historical tourism
- Ancestral research
- Spiritual journeys
- Academic study

The homeworld's symbolic importance far exceeds its political or economic weight in the modern era.
## Mars

Terraformed into an industrial hub. Mars represents successful planetary engineering on a massive scale, though its character has become thoroughly industrial rather than preserving any frontier romanticism.
## Synthesis

The Neo-Solar Republic's capital station occupies the Sol-Earth L5 Lagrange point. See [Neo-Solar Republic](../powers/neo-solar-republic.md) for details.
## Significance

Sol's importance is largely symbolic and historical. The system is not a major economic or political power, but its status as humanity's origin grants it unique cultural weight.
