# The Sol System

Humanity's origin system: a mix of pilgrimage site, industrial hub and radical experiment. Its importance is largely symbolic and historical, and it is not a major economic or political power.

## Earth

Governed by the United Nations, one of the few surviving pre-Scattering political structures. Earth is a destination for historical tourism, ancestral research, spiritual journeys and academic study. Its symbolic importance far exceeds its political or economic weight.

## Mars

Terraformed into an industrial hub—planetary engineering on a massive scale, accomplished centuries before the Elysian Collective existed, which the Collective's histories handle with careful respect. Its public character is industrial: shipyards, refineries, the grimy pride of the oldest working world. Up close, eight centuries of settlement have left it as various as any old country, with preserved landing-site parishes, agricultural poles, and quarters that have always resented the foundry image.

## Synthesis

The Neo-Solar Republic's capital station occupies the Sol-Earth L5 point, and most of the system's population with it.
