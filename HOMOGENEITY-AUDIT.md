# Homogeneity Audit — flags for the variety pass

*Compiled 3862 C / 2026-07-10. Scope: every file in `setting/`, `powers/`, `worlds/`, `appendices/` (major files read in full; location files swept for universalizing patterns). Purpose: flag wording that makes universal claims, adds uniformity, or shrinks the sense of fractal variety — even as you zoom in, people should not all be the same.*

**STATUS: applied, same day.** All Tier 1–3 flags were rewritten in the working tree (uncommitted). Doctrine established by the fixes, for future consistency: the **quid** is the corridor standard, not a universal currency; **Trade Tongue** is the corridors' lingua franca with rivals and gaps; there is **no galactic religious rule** — the corridors run secular and off-corridor worlds vary wholesale; **Arrhenos** works a genuinely *niche* trade — the neutral keeping of money that must survive its own politics (war chests, dynastic reserves, arbitration stakes) — in which it is the first name and the great powers' default but *not* alone (smaller neutral houses work the same seam regionally), while regional houses do the galaxy's daily banking and most interstellar commerce never touches an Arrhene ledger; the galaxy's stereotypes of Arrhene men include both the naif and (unfairly, fed by the reactionary fringe) the misogynist; the **Freeholds** depend on *outside* banking under many flags, with Arrhene houses merely the reputable example of the leash; the Arrhene **diaspora** has a thin, real second generation — children fathered with local partners or adopted, whom the homeworld's registries have no line for (the Continuity is only the homeworld's method; Arrhene men are not infertile); the **Elysian Collective** monopolises only the full art (dead rock to open sky) above a stratum of lesser outfits and leaked methods; **Vega media** is the prestige import, not the wallpaper of the species, and Vega's own trillion are several audiences; **longevity** (~200 yr) reaches only where the medicine does; **Parvati/Osthavn** serve the throne world (plus a prestige tier), not the empire; Arrhenos is the largest of the galaxy's **gender experiments**, not the only one, and the naif stereotype is the most common reception, not the standing one. Left deliberately unchanged: already-hedged lines (weddings "very nearly universal", the boyhood ladder, diaspora "will always be asked"), comparative superlatives, and the Tiamming table "Character" rows.*

**A note on what was NOT flagged.** The seven great-power files already do this work deliberately (Cartel "there is no such thing as Cartel culture"; Mandate "the least uniform thing ever to insist that it is uniform"; Drift "not one people"; NSR "true of perhaps a third of it"; Vega "the smallest part of Vega"; Elysian's three lives; Freeholds "no two alike"). Superlatives like "the largest fleet in human space" are comparative facts, not homogenizers, and were left alone. Rhetorical "the one thing / no one" constructions were left alone unless they make a world-claim.

## Tier 1 — Setting-wide singletons (the biggest levers)

These are the claims that make 4,000 systems, ~3,000–4,000 polities, and months-long communication lag feel like one integrated market with one of everything. The setting's own physics (`setting/technology/ftl/communication.md`: "There is no galactic 'now'... every system inhabits its own informational light-cone") argues *against* every one of them; the fix is usually to make the singleton the **corridor standard** or the **prestige tier of many**, not the only one.

### 1. The Quid — one universal currency
- `setting/trade-and-currency.md:5` — "The Quid is the **universal fiat currency of human space, recognised across all major and most minor polities**."
- `setting/trade-and-currency.md:7` — "the Quid is the **universal standard**"
- `appendices/conversion-tables.md:28` — "The quid is the **universal standard**"
- Why it flattens: one fiat currency maintained across thousands of sovereign polities with no FTL signalling is both implausible and homogenizing — and line 7 already contains the better idea ("minor polities and frontier systems are typically awash in multiple overlapping monetary standards"). Let that be the whole truth: the quid as the dominant *trade/reserve* standard of the great corridors, issued/cleared differently region by region, trading at wild discounts in volumes where no one will honour a puck, with rival standards in some reaches.

### 2. Trade Tongue — one universal language
- `setting/culture.md:7` — "a **universal lingua franca**... functions as a **nearly ubiquitous** second language for anyone interacting across star systems."
- Why it flattens: same physics problem, same fix. Ubiquitous *along the trade corridors*; thin to absent off them; regional trade pidgins and rival linguae francae in other volumes of space; mutually strange dialects of Trade Tongue itself after 1,100 years (Ascendant Drift, which evolved *out* of it, is the model already in canon — `powers/drift-communities/haven-ascendant/society.md:33`).

### 3. Uniform secularism — one religious temperature for all humanity
- `setting/culture.md:13` — "**The vast majority of humanity** maintains no strong religious commitment... these are **notable exceptions rather than the galactic rule**."
- Echoed nearly verbatim in every power: `powers/vega-throne/culture.md:41` ("as across most of human space"), `powers/union-freeholds/culture.md:23` ("secular like most of human space"), `powers/mandate/culture.md:23` ("Most citizens and subjects carry no strong religious commitment"), and Arrhenos is secular by design.
- Why it flattens: that *every* surveyed society lands on the same majority-secular setting is exactly the uniformity the file says doesn't exist for governance (`setting/culture.md:17` proudly lists seven models of government — then gives religion one model). Thirteen centuries of isolated diaspora should breed new faiths constantly, not just preserve "tolerated old" ones. Options: reframe the claim as the view from the cosmopolitan corridors (where the compilers of this document live); seed whole devout regions, young religions, syncretisms, and secular worlds that are secular in *different ways*. The one gesture currently existing — "several worlds heavily restrict or entirely ban enhancements due to deeply held cultural or religious convictions" (`setting/technology/enhancement.md:25`) and "the few worlds that still hold strong religion" (`worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md:38`) — treats faith as a rare curiosity.

### 4. Arrhenos — the setting-wide banking monopoly
The chain of claims, strongest first:
- `worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md:43` — "Arrhenos became what it remains: **not *a* financial centre of human space but *the* one, indispensable and unchallenged**." (Same paragraph implies its only rival ever was one Vega house.)
- `setting/trade-and-currency.md:23` — "Arrhenos: **The capital of galactic financial services** and neutral banking."
- `worlds/arrhenos/index.md:12` — "Premier banking centre"; `economy.md:9` — "**Synonymous with** secure, neutral banking. The Consortium **dominates** the planetary economy and shapes Arrhenos's role in human space."
- `worlds/arrhenos/economy.md:27` — "**Quadrillions of quid pass through the planet daily**." `economy.md:49` / `military.md:38` — "**no major power can afford** to see Arrhenos destabilised—disruption would **cascade through galactic financial systems**."
- Repeated from other vantages: `powers/sable-cartel/index.md:25` ("the one financial centre the Cartel cannot do without"), `powers/union-freeholds/index.md:53` ("the indispensable banker"), `powers/mandate/index.md:63`, `powers/drift-communities/overview.md:29`.
- Why it flattens: one planet clearing the galaxy's money across months of lag shrinks 4,000 systems to a single town with a single bank. Canon already contains the correction: **Nexus "lends where no one else will—to stations and settlements and small polities Arrhenos would not return a call from"** (`powers/sable-cartel/fronts.md:23`), and Vega ran a "genuine rival" until 3839. Suggested shape: Arrhenos is the most *trusted* name — the neutral arbiter and the prestige tier of cross-power, high-stakes finance — while the actual volume of human-space banking runs through thousands of regional houses, exchanges, and cooperatives it does not touch and could not reach. "Unchallenged" can stay true *of its niche* (neutral banking between great powers) without being true of finance as such; Kraeven was the rival *in that niche*. The Banking War file's "the unrivalled banker of human space" (line 3) and the "quadrillions daily" figure are the load-bearing sentences to soften.
- Bonus internal tension: `economy.md:53` — "Client Confidentiality: **Absolute**." vs. `banking-war.md:25` — "It **mostly** held. There were breaks..." The short file states as a law what the long file richly complicates; let the short file carry the hedge ("sold as absolute; the breaches are famous precisely because they are few").

### 5. Elysian Collective — the terraforming monopoly
- `setting/overview.md:30` — "**Unrivalled** terraforming specialists"
- `powers/elysian-collective/index.md:5` — "**Every breathable sky humanity did not find but *made***—every engineered ecosystem, every nudged orbit—came from Collective hands or Collective methods."
- `powers/elysian-collective/index.md:12` — "sold at **monopoly prices**"; `index.md:19` — "**Every polity** that wants a new habitable world **needs the Collective**... it simply owns **the one door the future has to pass through**."
- Why it flattens: a 40-million-person guild with a 1,300-year absolute monopoly across 10,000 worlds is the same one-shop-galaxy problem as Arrhenos banking. "or Collective methods" is already doing quiet work — press on it: methods leak, licensees exist, journeymen wash out and sell what they know, cut-rate local outfits do crude dome-to-suit work the guild wouldn't dignify with the word terraforming. The Collective's real monopoly can be the *hardest* tier — full Terrene transformations and Lazarus revivals — plus the brand, the world-songs, the naming. Mars, incidentally, was terraformed before the Collective plausibly existed (`worlds/sol-system.md:17`), which is already a crack in "every breathable sky."

### 6. Vega — one media culture for all human space
- `worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md:3` — "[Vega's] entertainments, **which saturate human space**"
- `powers/mandate/index.md:58` — "Vega entertainment **saturates the Mandate** faster than the Bureau can ban it"; `powers/mandate/culture.md:37` — "**devoured across the Mandate**"
- `powers/vega-throne/index.md:3` — "**the largest audience in human space**"; `powers/vega-throne/culture.md:19` — "**Everyone** watches Vega media; **everyone** has opinions on the current serials; celebrity gossip is **the universal second language**."
- Why it flattens twice: galactically, one entertainment industry papering 4,000 lagged systems homogenizes everywhere at once (the Freeholds file has the right texture — serials "arrive years late" and the frontier reads them differently); domestically, "everyone watches" makes 1.4 trillion people on multiple worlds one audience. Canon hook already present: "the agricultural worlds the court forgets between harvests" (`powers/vega-throne/index.md:29`) surely have their own scenes, stars, and serials the court has never heard of. Vega as the *prestige import* — the thing local industries imitate, pirate, and resent — is bigger and stranger than Vega as the wallpaper of the species.

### 7. Enhancement and lifespan — one baseline for all humanity
- `setting/technology/enhancement.md:7` — "the average life expectancy is **approximately 200 years**... **most humans (even those who are non-augmented) barely age visibly** past their fortieth year."
- Why it flattens: the file's own header says adoption "varies wildly," and the Mandate emperor's youth is "available to anyone **who can afford them**" (`powers/mandate/index.md:29`) — but the baseline claim hands 200 years to everyone from the Radiant Court to the Provisional Territories and the refugee havens. Making longevity *unevenly distributed* (by wealth, by region, by choice) is a huge variety lever: worlds where 200 is normal next to worlds where 90 still is, and the moral weather between them.

### 8. The one-of-each trade-hub table
- `setting/trade-and-currency.md:20–23` — "Tethys-Var: **The absolute center** of the galactic fez... Vega System: **The premier hub** for luxury goods and media... Arrhenos: **The capital** of galactic financial services."
- Why it flattens: a galaxy with exactly one fez town, one media town, one bank town. "Absolute center" also contradicts canon: Tethys-Var supplies **13.5%** of the harvest and 85% comes from thousands of scattered minor sources (`setting/technology/fez.md:17–18`, `worlds/tethys-var.md:15`). Suggested register: "largest single source," "the most prestigious of several," etc. Same instinct for the three named trade routes: fine as the famous ones, worth a line acknowledging the thousands of lesser circuits (the *Polletio*'s fourteen-month loop implies them).

### 9. The C Calendar
- `setting/calendar.md:3` — "serves as **the standard timekeeping system across human space**"; `calendar.md:17` — "**Dawn typically occurs** around the 5th hour..."
- Mild flag: the regional-variations paragraph at the end already concedes parallel local calendars; the "dawn" line quietly assumes every world's day matches the 20-hour standard (canon says Arrhenos had to *adjust its orbit* to comply — most worlds can't). Worth one clause: on unsynchronised worlds the standard day is bookkeeping, and local life runs on local light.

## Tier 2 — Single stereotypes held by everyone

The user-flagged pattern: one attitude assigned to a whole population, or one reputation assumed to greet a person everywhere they go.

### 10. The single galactic stereotype of Arrhene men
- `worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md:3` — "**The standing assumption off-world** is that a man from a world without women must be naive about them—sheltered, fascinated, easily startled."
- `worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md:29–31` — "the distance between **what the gendered galaxy expects of him** and what he actually is... he was never **the naif they assumed**... fielding **for the hundredth time** the question of *what your women are like*."
- `worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md:25` — "A people **known for** keeping secrets and taking no side **will always be asked**..."
- `worlds/arrhenos/locations/verenstad-gamma/gamma.md:37` — "Arrhene men are aware of **this reputation**" (the definite article assumes one).
- Why it flattens: the file itself brilliantly varies *Arrhene* attitudes ("There is no single Arrhene attitude to women, only a spread") and varies views of *the planet* (the five-row table) — but views of the *men* still run through one galaxy-wide script (the naif), and the diaspora file makes "the expat condition" one condition. In a fractal galaxy the reception should vary by where he lands: ports that see so many Arrhene clerks that the novelty wore off a century ago; regions that know Arrhenos only as a bank and are surprised to learn about the gender part; societies whose own gender arrangements make Arrhenos unremarkable (see #11); places that have never heard of it and just met a banker with a strange accent; and yes, the prurient and the awed. The "standing assumption" can survive as the *most common* or the *most annoying* reception rather than the standing one.
- Related, smaller: `women-and-gender.md:17` — the Trade-Tongue gender-handling "tell... **as good as a passport**" universalizes every Arrhene speaker; diaspora bankers who've spent thirty years in Vega presumably pass. "To people who know the accent" already hedges; one more clause would do it.

### 11. Arrhenos as the galaxy's only gender experiment
- `worlds/arrhenos/index.md:3` and throughout — Arrhenos is framed (and received) as *the* single-gender society, singular in human space.
- Why it flattens: across 10,000 settled worlds and 1,300 years, the Endeavour Fleet is unlikely to be the only such experiment — all-female worlds, post-gender societies, worlds with five recognised genders, failed and small versions of all of these. Even one line acknowledging cousins (and Arrhene opinions *about* those cousins) would enlarge the galaxy and give the "refuge" row of the outside-view table more to point at. (This is additive world-building rather than a wording fix — flagged because the *uniqueness claim* is itself a homogenizer about everyone else.)

### 12. "How the frontier sees its neighbours" — 1.7 trillion, one opinion
- `powers/union-freeholds/culture.md:37` — "**It holds** the Mandate in contempt... **It regards** Arrhenos with pragmatic respect... **It finds** Vega soft and performative... **it feels** a real kinship with the Drift."
- Why it flattens: the singular "it" gives four thousand radically different Freeholds one foreign policy of the heart. The Freeholds must hold *billions of Mandate emigrants* alone (canon: fleeing subjects find a door here) whose feelings about the Mandate are anything but contempt. Reframe as the median bar-room take, with the file's own habit of naming who dissents.

### 13. Visitor-reaction one-liners (one hat per polity)
- `powers/vega-throne/culture.md:63` — "**Arrhene visitors**... struggle with the basic premise; **Mandate visitors** recognise hierarchy but not its fusion with the market; **Drift travellers**... find the scale and formality overwhelming."
- Same pattern in miniature wherever powers describe each other's citizens. The relations *tables* are fine (states can have policies); sentences about what *visitors from X* feel assign one inner life per passport. The existing hedge ("in proportions that say as much about the visitor as the place") is good — the per-polity clauses could each carry a "tend"/"often," or better, one contrary example.

## Tier 3 — Fractal failures: zoom in and everyone's the same

### 14. Tiamming's one-function continents
- `powers/mandate/locations/tiamming/parvati.md:3` — "**Parvati makes what the Mandate consumes**... where **the empire** builds its ships, forges its weapons, processes its materials"; `parvati.md:10` — "Character: Industrial, manufacturing, extraction."
- `powers/mandate/locations/tiamming/osthavn.md:3` — "**Osthavn feeds the Mandate**... grows the food that supplies the throne world and ships the surplus **across the empire**."
- `powers/mandate/locations/tiamming/xuan.md` — the aristocratic north.
- Why it flattens, twice: **(a) scale** — a continent of 7 billion cannot industrially supply an empire of 2.4 trillion across hundreds of worlds, nor 3 billion farmers feed it; these sentences quietly shrink the empire to its throne world. Truer and better: Parvati builds *the fleet's prestige yards / the throne world's industry*; Osthavn feeds *Tiamming* (interstellar bulk food shipping is also economically strange). **(b) uniformity** — one continent = one industry = one "character" is the planet-of-hats pattern at continent scale. The file *interiors* already resist it (Osthavn holds automated plains, the eight-century Sandholm families, the Provisional Territories, Port Yelling; Parvati holds extraction zones, coastal belts, veterans, Wen Daoshi) — it's the thesis sentences and the one-word "Character" rows that overclaim. A continent with an industrial *reputation* still contains fishing coasts, university towns, hill shrines, resort valleys, and neighbourhoods that have never seen a foundry.

### 15. The Mandate's three cuisines
- `powers/mandate/culture.md:39–47` — "**Three traditions**, three philosophies of what food is for" — Xuanese, Parvatan, Osthavn.
- Why it flattens: the empire-wide culture file maps all Mandate food onto the throne world's three continents — 2.4 trillion people, hundreds of worlds, 200+ languages, three cuisines. Easy reframe: these are the three *court-recognised* traditions (the ones Classical poetry has words for), floating on thousands of regional kitchens the Court has never tasted.

### 16. Empire-wide universal customs
- `powers/mandate/culture.md:84` — "The traditions of the guest run deep and **cross every other line**. A guest is sacred... It is **the one relation in the Mandate that the citizen-subject distinction does not touch**."
- `powers/mandate/culture.md:69` — Misrule: "**none** has managed to abolish it."
- Why it flattens: one hospitality code and one festival held uniformly across 200 language-communities and hundreds of worlds. Both are lovely; both would be lovelier as *official* universals with uneven realities — hospitality as the ideal everyone cites and particular regions honour very differently; Misrule kept fervently some places, perfunctorily others, fused with older local festivals elsewhere (the regional-variations section already starts this — extend the instinct past Tiamming).

### 17. One-hat planets in the worlds files
- `worlds/sol-system.md:17` — Mars's "character has become **thoroughly industrial** rather than preserving any frontier romanticism." A terraformed planet with one adjective; even one clause of internal variety (preserved landing-site districts, agricultural poles, the neighbourhoods that despise the foundry image) would help.
- `worlds/unique-worlds.md` — Awyr's low gravity "**fundamentally shapes** Awyr's architecture, transportation, social customs, and sense of space" (one environmental fact → one society). These are stubs; flag is a caution for when they're expanded. (Periapt's "two parallel civilisations" is already the right instinct — an environment producing *more* variety, not less.)

### 18. Arrhenos micro-universals (each small; they add up)
- `worlds/arrhenos/culture.md:49` / `society.md` — "**All Arrhene languages** have only one set of animate pronouns." On a "deeply multilingual" planet whose languages evolved from *different Earth stocks*, total convergence is suspicious. "Nearly all" plus one vestigial exception (a liturgical register, a Zespol dialect that kept gendered forms for foreigners and animals) is more lived-in and gives the linguists something to argue about.
- `worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md:21` — "the same institution appears first, **every time**: a bathhouse." Charming, and could stay; "almost always, and the exceptions get teased for it" is charming *and* fractal.
- `worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md:19` — commercial intimacy clusters in "a quarter of **every** spaceport city."
- `worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md:51` — weddings: "The reflex is **very nearly universal**" (hedged — borderline; fine to keep if the exceptions exist somewhere).
- `worlds/arrhenos/society.md:63` — torcs "**ubiquitous across Arrhenos**" (a planet of 2.1 billion with zero torc-refusers? one line about the bare-necked — eccentrics, a Zespol habit, a philosophical fringe — would do it).
- `worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/index.md:20` — "Rooftop gardens are **universal**." City-scale, low stakes; still the tic.
- `worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md:23` — "a recognised ladder—**the same in outline across the planet**, however differently each city dresses it" (the hedge is doing good work here; flagged only for awareness).

## Tier 4 — Internal contradictions the variety pass can exploit (not fix)

Canon already contains the seeds of the less homogeneous galaxy; these pairs point the direction:

1. **Tethys-Var "absolute center"** (`setting/trade-and-currency.md:20`) vs. **13.5% of supply, 85% from thousands of scattered sources** (`setting/technology/fez.md:17`). The fez file is the model — keep its numbers, fix the table's rhetoric.
2. **Arrhenos "the one financial centre"** (`worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md:43`) vs. **Nexus lending across a frontier "Arrhenos would not return a call from"** (`powers/sable-cartel/fronts.md:23`) and Vega's Therenne house running finance right now (`powers/vega-throne/index.md:56`). The galaxy demonstrably has other banks; the monopoly language just forgets them.
3. **"Absolute" client confidentiality** (`worlds/arrhenos/economy.md:53`) vs. **"It mostly held. There were breaks"** (`worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md:25`). Let the reference file match the narrative file.
4. **"Every breathable sky... came from Collective hands"** (`powers/elysian-collective/index.md:5`) vs. **Mars, terraformed under Sol authority** (`worlds/sol-system.md:17`) and the Cascade-era timeline. At least one pre-Collective terraforming tradition existed; where did it go? (That's a story, not a bug.)
5. **"There is no galactic 'now'... every compiled document carries the caveat that some contents may be out of date"** (`setting/technology/ftl/communication.md:66`) vs. every "universal / everyone / saturates human space" claim above. The physics file is the setting's best argument for its own variety — the universals contradict it.

## Suggested order of work

1. `setting/trade-and-currency.md` + `setting/culture.md` + `appendices/conversion-tables.md` — the three files that declare the universals (quid, Trade Tongue, secularism, the one-of-each hub table). Small edits, biggest radius.
2. The Arrhenos monopoly chain (`banking-war.md:3,43`, `economy.md`, `index.md:12`, `trade-and-currency.md:23`, plus the echo lines in other powers' relations tables) — decide the doctrine ("most trusted neutral tier of many," not "the only bank") and apply it consistently.
3. The Arrhene-stereotype pair (`women-and-gender.md:3`, `diaspora.md:29–31`, `gamma.md:35–37`) — vary the reception by destination; optionally seed cousin societies (#11).
4. Elysian monopoly softening (`overview.md:30`, `elysian index.md:5,12,19`).
5. Vega saturation + "everyone watches" (`vega culture.md:19` and the cross-file "saturates" lines).
6. Tiamming continent frames + Mandate three-cuisines/hospitality (`parvati.md:3`, `osthavn.md:3`, `mandate/culture.md:39–47,84`).
7. Enhancement/lifespan distribution (`enhancement.md:7`).
8. Tier-3 micro-universals as a sweep.
