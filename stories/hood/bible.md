## Locked decisions (Book 1)

The forks settled with the owner; the arc rests on these.

- **The System is a genuine SF mystery.** Something real crossed over; Milo's arrival was [*made*, not random](protagonist.md); the [anomalies are true clues](system.md). In-text ambiguity is a deliberate veil, not real uncertainty. Book 1 cracks the door without explaining.
- **Milo's edge is systems/pattern literacy** — the faculty that earns his [board job](outline.md) and makes him useful to the runners; the [System rendered loud](system.md).
- **Lex is canon, and a friend — not the love interest.** [Alexios Makris-Webb](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in), 19, the [Westslope Gardens](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md) household's eldest; bored, clever, [building something secret in the workshop](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md). He ropes Milo into [running](cast.md#the-runner-network) as a reckless thrill, not a cold patsy plan, and his arc is recklessness → responsibility ([the brothers he "hasn't noticed" he's responsible for](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md)).
- **The romance is a separate adult: [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest).** A 30s [Shambles fixer](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) who first warns Milo off; the bond grows the [canon way](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md) (shared competence, understatement); its engine is that [secrecy is the only Arrhene betrayal that counts](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md), so Milo's hidden origin is the wound that could undo it.
- **Book 1 ends on intrigue resolved at a cost, secret intact.** Milo is roped in via his abilities and connections, helps resolve or escape it (friends — and the [Makris-Webb family](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) — in danger), pays a price (the phone gone; attention drawn) — and is **still hiding the truth of his origins from everyone, [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) and [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) included.** See [outline.md](outline.md).
- **Antagonist chosen and woven:** the [agritech-lending data theft + Cartel (Blackwell) shadow](cast.md#antagonist-position); only its visible face stays open.
- **The antagonist faction behind the hot drive is left open** — choose to set Book 2's lean ([candidates in cast](cast.md)).

## Plants awaiting payoff (loaded guns)

- **The Earth phone** — mint, anomalous, in [Sorian's](cast.md) keeping. The biggest gun on the board; **fires at the [climax](outline.md)** (spent / taken / destroyed) and leaves Sorian a loose end for Book 2.

- **The hot data-drive** — the intrigue's MacGuffin; whose it is = the [open antagonist choice](cast.md). Let its contents resonate, faintly, with Milo's origin.
- **Lex's secret workshop project / odd hours / silenced pings** — [canon](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md) ("building something secret in the workshop"); the double life before it's named.
- **Devan's own secret** — why he went quiet and stays low; the mirror to Milo, and the second reckoning loaded under the romance.
- **The Trade-Tongue tell** — Milo, posing as a Drifter, has [century-wrong grammar leaking through](setting-brief.md); a slow exposure risk for anyone attentive (a barber? a scholar? Sorian?).
- **Bram Drummond's Earth-cuisine counter** — [reconstructs the very food Milo remembers](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md): an uncanny near-miss; potential confidant or exposure.
- **The wish** — Milo's Earth-side desire for "clear rules / a reset button", granted with cruel literalness; pay off thematically at the climax.

## Timeline (in-story)

Pacing is now act-level, not the old day-and-a-half montage (see [renovation note](outline.md#renovation-note)).

- **Denouement — The Foothold.** A grey but real place; the secret holds; he chooses to stay; one deliberate move on the System.

## Tonal contract (don't violate)

Wry and warm over a real ache; comedy at the world's strangeness, never cruelty toward Milo's coping; cold numbers vs warm people, human register wins on collision. The setting stays a [civilised banking republic](setting-brief.md), not a prison planet; the [romance is the unremarkable default](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md). The metaphysical question (was the System ever real?) stays open; the human one (does Milo let himself be known?) gets answered.
