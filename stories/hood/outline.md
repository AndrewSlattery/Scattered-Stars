# Outline — Book 1

The scaffold for the first complete arc. It supersedes the original draft beats, which were diagnosed as too frictionless to build on (see [the renovation note](#renovation-note) below). Treat this as the working structure: directions and pressures, not a railroad. Apply [scene-and-sequel rhythm](craft.md) and the "[therefore / but](craft.md)" test throughout — every beat should follow from the last by causation or complication.

This is **Book 1 of an extended series**. It resolves Milo's survival arc and one self-contained intrigue; it deliberately *opens* the series' larger mysteries rather than closing them. Locked creative decisions feeding this arc are recorded in the [bible](bible.md).

## Renovation note

The original draft ran as a frictionless montage of successes — across a day and a half Milo was healed, torc'd, befriended, funded, housed and job-led, all smoothly, all *received*. The two engines the spec sells (the bare-neck disadvantage, the money problem) were defused within a scene each; Lex was contrived generosity with no motive; the mint phone was a one-object windfall that erased the financial stakes; the cover was asserted but never tested. The renovation keeps every good beat and re-tensions it:

- **Slow the gratification.** The torc-gap *bites* before it eases; money stays scarce; nothing is simply handed over.
- **Give the helpers motives.** [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) — canon's bored, clever nineteen-year-old, [building something secret in the workshop](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md) — ropes Milo in as a deniable accomplice for a [runner](cast.md#the-runner-network) thrill he only half understands. [Sorian](cast.md) keeps the phone because it fascinates him — a hook, not a windfall. The romance is a *separate adult*, [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest).
- **Make the lie dangerous and make it persist.** The [Drifter cover](protagonist.md) is pressured all book and **still holds at the end** — the secret is a series-length bomb, not a Book-1 reveal. On Arrhenos, [secrecy is the betrayal that ends bonds](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md), so Milo's hidden origin is precisely the wound that could undo the [romance](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest).
- **Make the System a driver.** Milo's [systems/pattern-literacy](system.md) is what makes him *useful*, not just deniable; the [anomalies](system.md) are real clues to a genuine mystery, and one of them is the decisive tool at the climax.
- **Converge the loaded guns.** The phone, the cover, the debt and the intrigue collide in one climax with a real cost, instead of resolving separately and early.

## Act I — Boot Up (renovated)

Arrival through the first precarious foothold. The goal of the act: get Milo from *cannot survive here* to *can, barely, and at a price* — and quietly assemble the intrigue around him. The beats below are the spine; **[act-1-storyboard.md](act-1-storyboard.md) opens each one into chapter-by-chapter blocking** (with [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in), [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest), the [Makris-Webb household](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) and the [woven antagonist](cast.md#antagonist-position) in place).

### 0. Earth — the wish, and the wrongness
Establish Milo's flat, unrewarding life and the one thing he's genuinely good at: reading systems, flows, patterns — fluent in games, lost in life (see [protagonist.md](protagonist.md)). **Plant the wish** for legible rules and a reset button. The [crossing](protagonist.md) is the writer's choice and stays unexplained — but seed a faint *wrongness*: a sense, just before, that he didn't fall so much as get **pulled**, that something selected him. (Series seed; don't explain it.)

### 1. Initialisation — and the bare neck bites
The System boots against the [Shambles'](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) sensory overload; he hides his neck → the *Hood* class. **But the disadvantage is immediate and frightening:** in a society where the [torc is identity, wallet and key](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md), a bare neck means he can buy nothing, pass nothing, prove nothing. He's moved along, refused, nearly flagged. Establish the gap as a real wall *before* anyone helps him over it.

### 2. The desk — interrogation, not service
The clinic/inquiry desk is a **hurdle**, not a kindness. Registering a torcless off-worlder triggers scrutiny; the [laggy interpreter](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md) is a hazard, not just colour. Under pressure he commits to the **robbed-[Drifter](../../powers/drift-communities/overview.md) cover** — get a detail wrong and he's detained. He's fitted a **placeholder torc**, but it's a *leash*: provisional, monitored, flagged for follow-up, gating nothing legitimate. The [biotech repair](../../setting/technology/enhancement.md) of his old injuries is real and quietly wondrous — but tied to the registration that now tracks him. He leaves more constrained, not less. **Therefore:** he must research the cover *while wearing it*, and stay ahead of a follow-up he can't pass.

### 3. No money, real desperation
The placeholder torc can't earn. Legitimate work needs a record he doesn't have; he tries and is turned away. He's hungry, broke, and the follow-up clock is ticking. *This* desperation — not convenience — is what makes him reachable. (Trim the old infodump: deliver the lost ~1,800 years and the Drift in dramatised scraps — a public feed he scans while loitering, a half-understood overheard argument — not a research montage.)

### 4. The bar — and Lex's read
He falls in with [the bar group](cast.md), cadging food from genuine need. **[Lex](cast.md) clocks him twice over:** a torcless, recordless, untraceable off-worlder — *and* sharp, because Milo, without thinking, reads a pattern in the room and wins a small bet / spots a cheat / calls a tram delay before the board does. Lex's interest sparks — not (only) warmth, *opportunity*: a deniable man who's also useful. He offers help (a torc, a stake, somewhere to sleep) with a hook in it. Milo, desperate, takes it — and is quietly using Lex too. **Two operators, each sure he's playing the other.**

### 5. The grey torc — into the underside
The **provisioned torc** comes not from a clean upmarket shop but through Lex's [grey connections](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) — customised, off-book, [HUD-integrating](system.md) (a real, deniable anomaly — note it). It works, and it deepens the leash: now Milo *owes Lex*, and his legitimacy is built on something hooky. This is his induction into the city's underside, where the rest of the book lives.

### 6. The phone & Sorian — a debt, not a windfall
Lex steers him to [Sorian](cast.md), expecting easy quid against the [pre-Scattering artefact](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md). Sorian is fascinated — *too* fascinated. The phone isn't just old; to a man who knows antiques, it is **impossibly** intact and quietly anomalous, and he knows it. He advances Milo a modest sum and **keeps the phone**, on strings — and now a shrewd, connected broker holds a mystery object and a set of questions. The money problem is *eased, not solved*; the most dangerous object on the planet is now in someone else's hands.

### 7. The board job, the warning, the family, the edge
Milo lands a **board job** — logistics/dispatch in the [Industrial District](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/industrial-district.md), reading the flow-boards that route the agritech belt's freight — where his [pattern-literacy](system.md) makes him uncannily, *visibly* good (his first competence-win that is genuinely **his**), and where he glimpses, without understanding, the [antagonist's footprint](cast.md#antagonist-position) in the data. The board also gives him the god's-eye flow-view the grey [runners](cast.md#the-runner-network) covet — how his honest job and the intrigue connect. Three more loaded scenes: [**Devan's** warning](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) (the romance's true first scene — an adult telling Milo he's exactly the kind of man who gets *spent*); [**lunch at the Makris-Webb home**](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md) (the warmth Milo could choose, and the family the danger will threaten — [Suren's](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) work-irregularity is the board footprint said aloud); and [**a night out**](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/nightlife.md) at the city's edge where running is pitched and the [spaceless inventory](system.md) proves, quietly, **real**. (Full blocking: [act-1-storyboard.md](act-1-storyboard.md) §8.)

**End of Act I:** a precarious foothold — a leashed torc, hooky debts to Lex and Sorian, a job, the cover holding by its fingernails — while the reader sees an intrigue gathering around Milo that he doesn't yet.

## Act II — Roped In

[Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) draws Milo into [running](cast.md#the-runner-network): un-logged physical couriering through the city's torc-blind seams, sold as easy money and a way to clear the debt. Milo's System renders it as stealth/heist play — patrol patterns, timing windows, objective markers — and he is *good*, his board-view and pattern-reading turning a deniable accomplice into an asset. Two threads grow in parallel: the **friendship with Lex** (reckless, exhilarating, and quietly one-sided in its honesty — Milo is lying to him too) and the **[romance with Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest)**, who keeps turning up where the work is and keeps not walking away. The romance builds the [canon way](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md) — shared competence, proximity, understatement — between two men each hiding what he is.

- **Midpoint — what Lex wanted, and what Devan is hiding.** Milo realises what a deniable, recordless man was *for*: Lex, in over his head, lined Milo up to carry the hot thing and, if it came to it, take the fall — a thing the kid arranged half-thinkingly and is now sick about, because the off-worlder has become a friend. Beside it, the [romance](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) deepens toward the point where Milo most wants to tell the truth and most can't — and a crack shows that **Devan has his own secret too**. Two reckonings loaded, neither sprung.
- **Escalation.** The thing being moved is concrete and resonant: a [physical data-drive](../../setting/trade-and-currency.md) of stolen [agritech-lending models](cast.md#antagonist-position) someone will kill to control — and the network behind it turns dangerous. Stakes widen to people Milo now cares about: the [Makris-Webb family](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) (whose [Suren](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) sits over the very data), [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest), [Sorian](cast.md) (whose phone-curiosity drew the same eyes). Develop the spine threads — [legitimacy, the phone, cover, the System](#act-i--boot-up-renovated) — don't let any go dormant.
- **The deeper seed.** The intrigue brushes, once, against something that resonates uncannily with *how Milo got here* — an artefact, a name, a pattern in the data that shouldn't mean anything to him and does. Plant it; **do not explain it.** (Series mystery — see [bible](bible.md).)

## Act III — The Run

Convergence. The intrigue comes to a head and the loaded guns fire together.

- **The crux — Lex grows up.** The danger comes home: [Milo](protagonist.md), [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest), and the [Makris-Webb household](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) — Lex's own fathers and brothers — are in the blast radius, and the easy out is to let the deniable off-worlder take the fall, as first arranged. **[Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) refuses** — spends *himself* instead, at real cost, finally [noticing the responsibility he's carried all along](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md). The reckless kid becomes, a year early and the hard way, the man canon says he's becoming. (The romance's own peak is separate and quieter: [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) endangered, and Milo choosing him — and a real life — over the safe distance of the System.)
- **Milo resolves it.** Using [pattern-literacy](system.md), his board-view, the deniability of a man no scan can place — and the **spaceless inventory**, the real anomaly finally decisive, to move or vanish the hot drive where no search can reach it — Milo engineers the resolution or the escape. The gamified mind, the thing that's kept the world at arm's length all book, is what saves the people he's stopped holding at arm's length.
- **The cost** (the ending you specified). They get out, but not clean. The **phone is gone** — spent, taken, or destroyed to buy them clear — and with it the simplest thread back to Earth and into [Sorian's](cast.md) keeping a loose, ticking end. Someone is hurt, or a bridge is burnt. And Milo is now *known*: a torcless ghost who did something remarkable has drawn the eye of dangerous people — and perhaps of whatever **pulled** him here. **The secret of his origins holds** — he is still lying to everyone, [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) and [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) included.

## Denouement — The Foothold

Milo has a real, if grey, place in the city: the board job, somewhere to live, a [friendship with Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) forged in fire, and a [romance with Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) that is now genuine — and everyone still keeping their deepest secrets (no one knows *what* Milo is; Milo hasn't told them; Devan hasn't told his own). He **chooses to stay** — or understands he cannot leave even if he wished to. The internal-arc beat: one deliberate movement on the System — he dismisses a habit he no longer needs, *or* lets himself believe one anomaly is real — the first real shift in the [gift / crutch / cage](system.md) question.

### Series engines left live for Book 2
- **The truth of his origins** — a bomb under the romance and the friendships, its fuse now lengthened, not cut.
- **The attention he's drawn** — dangerous people, and possibly Sorian, now know a torcless ghost did the impossible.
- **The pull** — the deepening evidence that his arrival was *made*, not random; that the anomalies are real; that he may not be the only one.

## Standing pacing notes
Don't rush Act II — the [setting's pleasures](setting-brief.md) (the alien-ordinary city, the un-fraught [romance](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md), the comedy of the double exposure) are the reason to stay, and the slow turn of instrumentality into love needs room. [Consolidate](craft.md) at each act break: what's planted, what's owed the reader, who's gone quiet. Keep the cast [small](cast.md) and [reincorporate before inventing](craft.md). The antagonist is chosen and woven — the [agritech-lending data theft and its Cartel shadow](cast.md#antagonist-position) — with only its *visible face* and the directness of the Cartel reveal left to pick, to suit how hard Book 2 should lean toward thriller.
