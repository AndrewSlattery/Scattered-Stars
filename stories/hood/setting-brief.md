# Setting brief — writing Arrhenos for *Hood*

This is **not** a lore document. The lore lives under [`worlds/arrhenos/`](../../worlds/arrhenos/index.md), [`setting/`](../../setting/overview.md), and [`powers/`](../../powers/index.md), and it is canon; this file holds only what a writer of *this story* needs to keep in hand — the tonal guardrails, the easy mistakes, and a touchpoints table pointing at the canon each story element draws on. When this brief and a canon file ever disagree, **canon wins** — fix the brief.

(An older standalone "Arrhenos writing guide" predates the vault and restated a lot of setting facts inline. Those facts now live in canon and have moved on; this brief deliberately keeps only the *guidance* and links out for the *facts*, so there is one source of truth.)

## The five tonal guardrails

These are the things easy to get wrong even with the lore open.

1. **It's a civilised banking republic, not a prison planet.** Arrhenos is [wealthy, stable, meritocratic, known for banking, art, and philosophy](../../worlds/arrhenos/index.md). Avoid testosterone-yard clichés — aggression, posturing, "alpha" tropes. Reach instead for [intellectual debate, elaborate etiquette, and emotional openness](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md); it is a safe place to be vulnerable, and a safe place to cry. Milo's Earth instincts will expect menace where there's only difference, and that misexpectation is good comedy — but the *world* shouldn't oblige him.

2. **The single-gender society is a deliberate structure, not a curse or a gimmick.** It was [chosen and engineered](../../worlds/arrhenos/index.md), and locals are [matter-of-fact about it](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md). Use [`he/him` for every Arrhene](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md); avoid female-coded terms (no "waitress", "actress", "queen") — the canon prefers neutral or fraternal forms ([server, performer, symposiarch, brother, father, spouse](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md)). **Never** plant a "hidden mother" or "long-lost sister"; the [Continuity Programme](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) is absolute and that twist breaks the world.

3. **The romance is the default, not a struggle.** [A man loving a man is as unremarkable as gravity here](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md) — no closet, no coming-out, no homophobia to overcome. The story's romantic obstacles are entirely human and entirely Milo's making (his secret, his distance, his stat-grinding habit). Don't import Earth's gay-narrative beats; the friction is internal. [Family structures are various](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md) — don't default to "two dads"; pick deliberately.

4. **No utopia.** Free of homophobia is not free of trouble. The real stakes are [wealth inequality](../../worlds/arrhenos/economy.md), [stifling bureaucracy](../../worlds/arrhenos/government.md), the [Axiomatic-vs-Romantic philosophical divide](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md), and above all **[loneliness](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md)** — isolation in a society of two billion. That last is Milo's whole emotional terrain; ground his stakes in *these* pressures, never in external prejudice.

5. **Mind the off-worlder reflex.** Canon is specific and counter-intuitive: Arrhenes are [not naive about women](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md) — they grew up [surrounded by them on-screen and in the news](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md), and meet them at [Gamma](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/verenstad-gamma/gamma.md) and abroad. The reaction to off-worlders is [mild interest or indifference, not shock](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md). So Milo's strangeness reads as *off-worlder, probably destitute* — unremarkable in kind — and the curiosity he draws is about his **bare neck and odd manner**, not exotic awe. The thing that genuinely marks an outsider is the [Trade-Tongue accent tell](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md): a lovely, usable hook for Milo's cover.

## Quick checklist (per instalment)

- [ ] [Pronouns](../../worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md): every Arrhene is *he* — no accidental *she*, no female-coded job titles?
- [ ] [Torc](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) present and doing work in the scene — and Milo's torc *status* correct on the [State Card](system-state-card.md)?
- [ ] [Time](../../setting/calendar.md) on the 20-hour clock with [Arrhene month names](../../worlds/arrhenos/index.md) (midday 10:00, midnight 20:00)?
- [ ] Money in [quid (ϟ)](../../setting/trade-and-currency.md)?
- [ ] [Family structure](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md) specific, not defaulted to nuclear?
- [ ] Emotional stakes grounded in setting pressures ([merit, money, bureaucracy, loneliness](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md)) rather than imported prejudice?
- [ ] Romance treated as the unremarkable default?
- [ ] [Botanical / architectural texture](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/index.md) dropped in to ground the alien-ordinary (purple-and-copper foliage, glowing translucent walkways, rooftop gardens, force-field fountains)?
