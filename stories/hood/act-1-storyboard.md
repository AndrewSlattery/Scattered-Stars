# Act I — Storyboard (beat-by-beat)

A working blocking of Act I, "[Boot Up](outline.md#act-i--boot-up-renovated)" — each [outline beat](outline.md) opened out into a chapter of granular beats: who is where, what they want, what turns, where the [double exposure](system.md) fires, and what it costs. This is scaffolding for prose, not prose; it commits to specifics so the writing has something to push against, and every specific here is overridable. Characters and canon are settled in [cast.md](cast.md), [protagonist.md](protagonist.md), and the [setting brief](setting-brief.md); the [bible](bible.md) holds live state.

**How to read a chapter.** Each has a purpose line, its setting/time/POV, a scene goal and unit type ([scene or sequel](craft.md)), the numbered beats, then a footer: *plants set · threads touched · State Card delta · the hinge out*. Inline tags: **▶** a System blue-box moment; **⚑** a plant loaded for later; **↪** the causal hinge ([therefore / but](craft.md)) into the next chapter.

**Cast shorthand:** Milo (~late 20s, Earth, our POV); **Lex** (Alexios, 19, the reckless friend — [canon](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md)); **Devan** (early-30s, the love interest, a [Shambles fixer](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest)); **Sorian** (the broker); the **Makris-Webb household** (Suren, Elio, Darien, Corin, Nikias). Antagonist: the [agritech-lending data theft](cast.md#antagonist-position) and its Cartel shadow, seeded but unseen in Act I.

## Chapter 1 — Earth: the wish, and the wrongness
*(outline Beat 0)* · **Purpose:** establish who Milo is and what he lacks, plant the wish, and make the crossing feel *aimed*.

**Setting/time/POV:** Earth, 2025, a grey ordinary day-into-night. Milo POV. **Goal:** show, don't tell, the gap between the man he is in games and the man he is in life. **Unit:** scene → sequel (the crossing is the disaster).

1. **The competence, wasted.** Open mid-flow: Milo doing the one thing he's good at — reading a system. Not a combat game; something with logistics, routes, markets, *patterns*. He's fast, fluent, three moves ahead. Establish [pattern-literacy](protagonist.md) as a felt thing, not a label.
2. **The life, a size too small.** Cut to the rest of it: the autopilot job, the unanswered messages, the flat where the screen is the warmest thing. He is hesitant, half a step behind, invisible. He knows the difference and it galls him.
3. **⚑ The wish, spoken.** Late, tired, half to himself or to a stranger online — Milo says the thing the universe is about to take literally: he wishes life came with *rules*. A scoreboard. A reset. Somewhere effort visibly counts. (Pay off at the [climax](outline.md).)
4. **⚑ The wrongness.** Just before the end, a glitch that shouldn't be there — a pattern in the game, or the lights, or the data that *answers back*, that seems to notice him. A half-second of being **reached for**. He tells himself he imagined it. (The series' founding gun — [do not explain](protagonist.md).)
5. **The crossing.** The method is the writer's choice and stays unexplained; what's fixed is the *cut*. Sensory whiteout — and the floor is wrong, the air is wrong, the smell is frying oil and wet stone.

*Plants:* the wish; the wrongness/"reached for"; pattern-literacy; whatever he's carrying (esp. the **phone**). *Threads:* origins (opened); the System (about to boot). *State Card:* none yet. *↪ Hinge:* he doesn't wake up home — **therefore** Chapter 2.

## Chapter 2 — Initialisation: the bare neck bites
*(outline Beat 1)* · **Purpose:** boot the System; make the [torc-gap](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) a wall *before* anyone helps him over it.

**Setting/time/POV:** a back half-alley in [the Shambles](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md), the wet roofed-but-not-walled kind; mid-afternoon, local. Milo POV. **Goal:** survive the first ten minutes without being detained. **Unit:** scene (goal: get oriented / get food; disaster: he can't do anything without a torc).

1. **Wake and overload.** Milo comes to against a damp wall on the [gradient](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) — purple-copper foliage in a rooftop gap above, force-field shimmer at a plaza edge, three levels of noise. Too much, too alien, too fast.
2. **▶ Boot.** The mind does its trick: an interface blinks up over the panic — a stat line, a flashing *??? LOCATION UNKNOWN*, an objective marker that just says **SURVIVE**. The gamification is born at peak incomprehension. Keep the first box small and shaky, not slick.
3. **The neck.** A passer-by's gaze snags on Milo's throat and slides away, wrong-footed. Milo doesn't get why — until he clocks that *everyone* wears a band of metal at the neck, and he doesn't. He pulls his hood up on instinct. **▶** *Class assigned: HOOD.* (He misreads a social catastrophe as a character build.)
4. **The wall, three times.** Quick escalating failures that teach the rule: he reaches for water at a fountain stall and there's no way to *pay* (no torc); he tries to follow others through a transit gate and it simply won't read him; a vendor's polite question turns cold when no ID pings back. Each "no" is a closed door, not a danger yet — but the doors are everywhere.
5. **Nearly flagged.** A municipal drone or a warden's torc pings him, gets nothing, and *pauses* — a beat of real fear, the System rendering it as a red **ALERT** he can't dismiss. He slips into the crowd-maze. The Shambles' [chaos](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md), for the first of many times, hides him.
6. **The only lead.** Hungry, soaked, hunted-feeling, he sees signage he can half-read (the [interpreter](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md) routine waking up) pointing to a clinic/inquiry desk. Help, or a trap. He has no other door.

*Plants:* the hood/class; the Shambles-as-refuge; the interpreter waking. *Threads:* legitimacy (the wall established); the System (booted). *State Card:* **first card appears** — Location: ??? / Torc: NONE (Social Stealth Disadvantage) / Class: Hood / Quest: SURVIVE. *↪ Hinge:* he can't survive on the street torcless — **therefore** he risks the desk (Chapter 3).

## Chapter 3 — The desk: interrogation, not service
*(outline Beat 2)* · **Purpose:** force the [Drifter cover](protagonist.md) into being under pressure; make the placeholder torc a leash.

**Setting/time/POV:** a Shambles clinic-and-inquiry desk (story location — functional, busy, half-medical half-bureaucratic). Late afternoon. Milo POV. **Goal:** get help without getting detained. **Unit:** scene (goal: pass as legitimate-enough; disaster: he's now registered and tracked).

1. **The queue, the read.** Milo watches the room first (his strength) — how people present torcs, what the attendant scans, which questions are routine and which sharpen. He's assembling a cover from observation in real time.
2. **First contact — the medic.** The attendant (the [medic](cast.md)) is brisk, not unkind. The [interpreter](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md) lags; Milo's answers arrive a beat late, which reads as concussion or shock — usefully. **▶** *Skill unlocked: Xeno-Linguistics (Lv 1).*
3. **The corner he's backed into.** No torc, no record, no country, odd speech. The medic's questions tighten toward *flagging him*. ⚑ Cornered, Milo reaches for the one story that fits a man with nothing: **robbed [Drifter](../../powers/drift-communities/overview.md) off-worlder** — tin-can havens, no records, put off a ship with nothing. He's improvising over an abyss; one wrong detail and it collapses.
4. **▶ The cover, gamified.** The System frames it as a dialogue tree — options lighting up, a **Deception check** he can't see the dice for. He picks the answers that draw least heat. It half-works: the medic's suspicion eases to *pity plus paperwork*.
5. **The body, casually mended.** A scan flags old Earth injuries — a badly-set bone, bad teeth, the wear of a hard ordinary life — and the clinic [fixes them as routine](../../setting/technology/enhancement.md), barely looking up. Milo is quietly wrecked by it: the future is *kind to bodies* in a way his world never was. (Warmth amid the fear; the first thing that makes him want to stay.)
6. **⚑ The leash.** He's fitted a **placeholder torc** — but it's conditional: provisional ID, monitored, flagged for follow-up, gating nothing legitimate. The medic, meaning well, points him "upmarket" for a real one and for clothes, and logs him for a check-in he knows he can't pass. **▶** *Torc: PLACEHOLDER (provisional — flagged). Quest added: pass the follow-up.*
7. **Out, and worse off.** He leaves mended and named and *tracked* — more constrained, not less, and still with no money and a clock now running.

*Plants:* the cover (improvised, fragile); the follow-up clock; the casual-kindness-of-medicine note. *Threads:* cover (opened); legitimacy (leashed); the System (Xeno-Linguistics started). *State Card:* Torc → PLACEHOLDER; Cover: intact (fragile); Skills: Xeno-Linguistics 1; Quest: pass the follow-up. *↪ Hinge:* a leashed torc can't earn and the clock is ticking — **therefore** the money problem bites (Chapter 4).

## Chapter 4 — Desperation: no money, no record, no door
*(outline Beat 3)* · **Purpose:** make scarcity real and make it the thing that renders Milo *reachable*.

**Setting/time/POV:** out across the Shambles plazas and half-alleys; dusk into evening. Milo POV. **Goal:** get money or food legitimately. **Unit:** scene → sequel (failure → the low point that primes Chapter 5).

1. **The failed legitimate paths.** Quick montage-in-scene: he tries to trade labour, to sell the literal clothes he arrived in, to get a meal on credit. Each needs a *record* he doesn't have. The meritocracy that should reward effort can't even *see* him. ⚑ (His [wish for a world where effort counts](protagonist.md) is being answered with cruel exactness.)
2. **Dramatised exposition, not a montage.** He delivers the lost ~1,800 years and the Drift in *scraps*, diegetically: a public feed he loiters under (half-understood, the [interpreter](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md) climbing); an overheard argument about lending and jobs; a child explaining a [torc](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) to a younger one, which teaches Milo more than any feed. No research-montage.
3. **The fountain.** Parched, he finally drinks from a public [fountain](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/fountain-plazas.md) — free, ubiquitous, the one thing the city gives without asking ID. A small mercy; a small dignity-cost. He watches the [Fountain Steps](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) crowd and learns the city by watching it navigate by water.
4. **The low point.** Night, cold, hungry, the follow-up clock in his gut. **▶** The System, with terrible cheer, posts *Hunger debuff* and an empty quest log. The joke lands as despair. He is exactly desperate enough to take a stranger's hand.
5. **Drawn toward warmth and noise.** Light and laughter from a bar up [Kellwell Lane](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md). Food smell. He drifts toward it with no plan but proximity.

*Plants:* the wish being answered cruelly; the fountain as the city's one free thing; hunger. *Threads:* money/survival (pressing); legitimacy. *State Card:* Cred: ~0 / Hunger debuff / Quest: SURVIVE still open. *↪ Hinge:* starving and out of doors, he walks into the one place that won't card him at the threshold — **therefore** the bar (Chapter 5).

## Chapter 5 — The bar: Lex's read
*(outline Beat 4)* · **Purpose:** introduce [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) and the bar group; let Lex clock Milo as *deniable and sharp*; start the two-way using.

**Setting/time/POV:** a small bar up Kellwell Lane, the entertainment strip; evening. Milo POV. **Goal:** get fed and stay unremarkable. **Unit:** scene (goal: cadge a meal, blend in; disaster — a *good* one — he gets noticed).

1. **In, and improvising.** Milo eases into the warmth of [the bar group](cast.md) — half a dozen men, easy banter. He cadges a round and snacks off them with a thin line about being newly off a ship. Hunger makes him a good listener; he soaks up grounding (work, [family](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md), the city) and fields idle Drift questions ("tin-can homes, *more than one gender*?") with improvised plausibility. Name one regular who'll recur — **Yannick**.
2. **Lex, testing everything.** [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) — nineteen, sharp-dressed in a [Landwick-cool](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md) way that's slightly too much for provincial Brovdingonai, half-bored — is the one who actually *looks* at Milo instead of past him. He's amused by the off-worlder; he's also bored and hunting for something interesting.
3. **⚑ The tell that hooks him.** Milo, without thinking, reads the room: calls a [tram](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/transit-hub.md) delay before the board flips, or spots a card-cheat, or finishes a pattern someone's been failing to. **▶** *Insight (passive).* The group laughs it off; **Lex doesn't.** A torcless ghost who can *do that* is exactly the kind of interesting Lex has been looking for — and exactly the kind of deniable he could *use*.
4. **The offer, with a hook.** Lex peels Milo off — buys him a proper meal, draws out the (false) story, and lands the offer: he knows a place for a real torc, can stake him, can put a floor under him tonight. Genuine warmth, real angle, and Lex nineteen-years-old certain he's the one running it.
5. **Milo, also calculating.** Milo takes it — and is reading Lex too: a rich kid playing at something, a useful door. **Two operators, each sure he's playing the other.** The reader sees both hooks; neither character does.
6. **⚑ The glimpse of the other life.** A message pings Lex's torc; he silences it too fast, glances at the time, mentions he has to "drop something" later. The double life, one beat, unnamed.

*Plants:* Lex's hidden errand; Milo's "tell" as his social currency; Yannick. *Threads:* Lex/intrigue (opened); the romance is *not* here. *State Card:* Relationship: **Lex — Wary→Intrigued**; Hunger cleared; Quest: get a real torc (via Lex). *↪ Hinge:* Milo accepts Lex's stake — **therefore** into the underside for the torc (Chapter 6).

## Chapter 6 — The grey torc: into the underside
*(outline Beat 5)* · **Purpose:** the [provisioned torc](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) via grey means (deepening the leash and the debt to Lex); first sighting of [Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest); the [HUD-integration anomaly](system.md).

**Setting/time/POV:** the lower, deep-interior Shambles — [torc specialists and "less official services"](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md); night. Milo POV. **Goal:** get a working torc. **Unit:** scene (goal achieved, but it costs).

1. **Down the gradient.** Lex leads Milo from the lit plazas down through arcades into the deep interior, threading the [maze](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) with a runner's unthinking ease (⚑ — Milo notes how *well* Lex moves the levels, files it away). The shift from outdoor-bright to enclosed-and-grey is the shift in the book's world.
2. **The torc-fitter.** A back-room specialist, no questions, cash-equivalent up front (Lex's). The torc is customised, off-book — passes casual scans, won't survive a real audit. Milo's now wearing legitimacy built on something hooky.
3. **▶ The integration anomaly.** As the torc seats and syncs, Milo's HUD *flinches* — the System and the torc handshake in a way that shouldn't be possible, the interface drinking the torc's data feed like it was waiting for it. **▶** *Interface linked.* A real, deniable clue; play it as a glitch he almost misses. (Note for the [mystery](system.md): the torc's [neural-interface](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) layer is the same tech Elio Webb works in.)
4. **⚑ First sight of Devan.** On the way out they pass a parcel-and-salvage desk wedged into the lower level — [Devan's](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest). Devan clocks the pair in one flat look: Lex he knows (and disapproves of); the torcless-now-grey-torc'd stranger beside him he reads in a heartbeat as *a man about to be spent*. A held beat, a word to Lex Milo doesn't catch, and they're past. (The romance's first frame: not a meet-cute, a warning sign.)
5. **The debt named.** Outside, Lex tots up what Milo owes — torc, meal, the floor. Friendly, but it's a *ledger* now. Milo needs money he still doesn't have. Lex, breezy: "There's the phone. And there's... other ways to make it back." ⚑ (The "other ways" = running, planted.)

*Plants:* Lex's runner-movement; the HUD/torc/neural-interface link; Devan (and his warning to Lex); the debt; "other ways." *Threads:* legitimacy (grey torc); the System (interface linked); Devan/romance (opened, cold); intrigue (looming). *State Card:* Torc → PROVISIONED (grey — fails real audit); Debt: owed-to-Lex; Skills note: Interface linked. *↪ Hinge:* he owes Lex and needs cash now — **therefore** the phone, and Sorian (Chapter 7).

## Chapter 7 — The phone & Sorian: a debt, not a windfall
*(outline Beat 6)* · **Purpose:** turn the phone from easy money into a [liability](cast.md#sorian--the-broker-who-knows-too-much); plug Milo into the antagonist's ecosystem without anyone naming it.

**Setting/time/POV:** [Sorian's](cast.md#sorian--the-broker-who-knows-too-much) antiques-and-curio shop, upmarket of the Shambles; late night / next morning. Milo POV. **Goal:** sell the phone for enough to clear the debt and live. **Unit:** scene (goal partially met; a worse problem opens).

1. **The walk up.** Lex steers Milo out of the Shambles to Sorian's, talking up an easy fortune for a [pre-Scattering artefact](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md). Milo's relief is premature.
2. **Sorian sees it.** Sorian handles the phone and *goes still*. He deals in genuine antiques; he knows in his hands that this is wrong — not period-replica, not even merely old, but **impossibly intact**, with a power cell that shouldn't hold and a fabric no catalogue lists. He powers it: it wakes (factory-reset, conveniently blank). His face does the thing a gambler's does.
3. **▶ Milo reads the read.** The System tags Sorian **Merchant → ??? (recalculating)** as his affinity colour shifts from green to amber — Milo's pattern-brain registering, before he reasons it, that he's just shown a dangerous man something he shouldn't have.
4. **⚑ The deal that's a hook.** Sorian doesn't pay a fortune. He advances a *modest* sum and **keeps the phone** — pawn-with-strings, redeemable at interest — because he wants to *study* it, and quietly find out what it is and who'd pay for the answer. Milo, needing the cash and unable to argue without drawing more attention, takes it. Half-solvent; the most dangerous object on the planet now in a curious stranger's safe.
5. **⚑ The ecosystem brushes past.** While they settle up, a beat of the wider world: Sorian takes a call, or a too-smooth associate is mid-deal at the counter — talk of *data*, of buyers, of something hot moving through the city. Milo doesn't register it; the reader files it. (The [antagonist's ecosystem](cast.md#antagonist-position), unnamed.)
6. **Squared with Lex, and indentured to the work.** Milo can now part-clear what he owes Lex — but he's spent the phone's leverage and still needs steady money. Lex, satisfied: come see what the "other ways" look like. The trap closes gently.

*Plants:* the phone in Sorian's keeping; Sorian's curiosity (the fuse); the data-buyer ecosystem; Milo's spent leverage. *Threads:* the phone (ticking); money (eased, not solved); intrigue (Milo now adjacent). *State Card:* Cred up (modest); Debt: phone → Sorian (interest), Lex part-cleared; Relationship: **Sorian — Wary**. *↪ Hinge:* still needing money, now curious about Lex's "other ways" — **therefore** the foothold day, and the descent toward running (Chapter 8).

## Chapter 8 — The foothold: the board, the warning, the family, the edge
*(outline Beat 7)* · **Purpose:** give Milo his first *earned* win, his first real warmth, and the act's closing image — a precarious foothold with the intrigue gathering. Four scenes.

### 8.1 — The board job
**Setting/time/POV:** an agritech dispatch floor, [Industrial District](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/industrial-district.md); day. Milo POV. **Goal:** hold down honest work. **Unit:** scene (a win — his first).

1. **The board.** A grey-torc-grade job nobody competent wants: watching the dispatch boards that route the agritech belt's freight — a wall of flows, schedules, and exceptions. To everyone else, drudgery. To Milo, *home*.
2. **▶ The translated competence.** His [pattern-literacy](system.md) lights the board up — he sees the rhythm, clears a snarl, predicts a bottleneck before it forms. **▶** *Skill: Logistics/Pattern-Reading — proficiency surging.* The first time competence is *his*, not received. A supervisor notices; a wary co-worker warms.
3. **⚑ The footprint.** In the flows, Milo's eye catches a wrongness he can't name — a lending-dataset that updates against the pattern, freight that routes to nowhere on the books. He flags it innocently; the response is a beat too quick, a touch too dismissive. (The [theft's signature](cast.md#antagonist-position); he's seen it before he understands it.)

*Plants:* the data footprint; Milo's value on the board. *Threads:* legitimacy (first honest money); the System (translated competence confirmed); antagonist (seeded). *State Card:* Cred: steady trickle; Skills: Logistics up; Quest: keep the job. *↪* **therefore** he can breathe — and gets curious about the wrongness, and about Lex's world.

### 8.2 — Devan's desk: the warning
**Setting/time/POV:** [Devan's](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) parcel-and-salvage desk, lower Shambles; that evening. Milo POV. **Goal:** (Milo) collect/learn something for Lex; (Devan) warn him off. **Unit:** scene (the romance's true first scene — friction, not flirtation).

1. **Crossed paths again.** An errand for Lex (or his own curiosity about the [board wrongness](#81--the-board-job)) puts Milo at Devan's desk. Recognition from [Chapter 6](#chapter-6--the-grey-torc-into-the-underside).
2. **The blunt warning.** Devan, dry and direct, tells Milo what he is in this world: a man with no record and no people, which makes him perfectly *disposable* — and that whatever Lex has him near is bigger and uglier than a bored teenager knows. Not unkind; the opposite. It's the most honest thing anyone's said to him here.
3. **⚑ The mirror.** Milo clocks that Devan, too, keeps his head down, his profile low, his past unmentioned — a man hiding in plain sight, which is why he reads Milo so fast. (⚑ Devan's own secret — the lying runs both ways.) Neither says it. The pull starts exactly here: in being *seen*, which the System can't do.
4. **▶ The number that's wrong.** The System posts Devan's affinity low and flat — *Wary* — even as the scene is the warmest, truest exchange Milo's had. **▶** The card is already lying about the one thing that matters. (Hold this divergence all series.)
5. **He doesn't take the warning.** Because he can't afford to. He needs the money; he needs Lex. He leaves rattled and seen and walks straight back toward the danger.

*Plants:* Devan's secret/mirror; the affinity-vs-truth gap. *Threads:* romance (opened, real); intrigue (the danger named, by Devan). *State Card:* Relationship: **Devan — Wary (per System)** [author note: the truth is already warmer]. *↪* **but** he ignores the warning — into the family that makes it all matter (8.3).

### 8.3 — Lunch at Westslope: the warmth and the stakes
**Setting/time/POV:** the [Makris-Webb home](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md), "the building"; midday. Milo POV. **Goal:** (Milo) a free meal and cover-maintenance; (story) make the family real enough to fear for. **Unit:** sequel (reaction/dilemma — a breather that quietly raises the stakes).

1. **The building, the warmth.** Lex brings Milo home like a stray. The communal building, the [roast dinner Elio takes pride in](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md), the easy non-rivalrous household — [Suren and Elio](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household), [Darien](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) over for the day, the brothers. Milo, who arrived more alone than anyone on the planet, sees an ordinary [Arrhene family](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md) up close. It undoes him a little. (The thing the System can't quantify; the life he could choose.)
2. **⚑ The uncanny meal.** Elio, delighted by an "off-worlder" guest, has reconstructed something *old*. Milo realises with a lurch he's eating a near-miss of food he actually remembers — eighteen centuries reconstructed and *almost* right. He can't react honestly without exposing himself. (Homesickness and exposure in one bite; cf. [Bram Drummond](cast.md).)
3. **⚑ Suren's unease.** Over the table, [Suren](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) is off — distracted, short. Drawn out, he mentions trouble at work: the [lending models](cast.md#antagonist-position) he runs are throwing irregularities, an audit's circling, the sector's jumpy. He means it as shop-talk. It's the *footprint Milo saw on the board*, said aloud at the dinner table. The reader connects it; Milo half does. The danger now has this family's address.
4. **⚑ Corin notices.** Sixteen-year-old [Corin](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) — quiet, watchful, the canon "likeliest confidant" — clocks the small wrongnesses in Milo (the beat-late laugh, the word he doesn't know, the way he watches exits). Says nothing. A look. (A future ally or exposure; loaded.)
5. **Nikias and Lex.** Eleven-year-old [Nikias](cast.md#the-makris-webb-household) idolises Lex; Lex is offhand-fond and oblivious to how much. ⚑ The canon line made flesh — Lex "has not yet noticed" he's responsible for these two. Milo sees it even if Lex doesn't. (The climax's stakes, planted at lunch.)

*Plants:* the family as stakes; Suren's irregularity = the board footprint; Corin's notice; Lex's unnoticed responsibility; the uncanny food. *Threads:* romance (Milo wanting a real life); antagonist (now tied to the family); cover (strained by Corin, by the meal). *State Card:* Relationships: household added (warm); Cover: strained; Tone: aching-warm. *↪* **therefore** Milo wants in to this world — and **but** the only way he can pay for it is the grey work, so: the edge (8.4).

### 8.4 — The night out: the edge, and the anomalies
**Setting/time/POV:** Brovdingonai [nightlife](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/nightlife.md) — comfortable bars shading into the city's seedier edge; night into the small hours. Milo POV. **Goal:** (Lex) show Milo the "other ways"; (Milo) decide. **Unit:** scene → the act's closing image.

1. **Out with the older crowd.** Lex and a rougher, older set than the bar group take Milo out — comfortable venues at first, then toward [the edge every city keeps](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/nightlife.md): a [commercial-intimacy](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md) strip, a back-room where the [runner network](cast.md#the-runner-network) does business. Milo meets the grey world properly.
2. **The pitch.** Lex lays out "running" as easy money and a thrill — moving things hand-to-hand through the city's [torc-blind seams](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md), un-logged, deniable. **▶** The System reframes it instantly as a *job board*: stealth contracts, patrol patterns, reward tiers. It looks, to Milo, like the one game he was ever good at. He's halfway sold before he decides.
3. **⚑ The anomaly, used.** A scare — a sweep, a pocket to ditch something hot, fast — and Milo, on instinct, drops an object into the [spaceless inventory](system.md). It vanishes from every scan. **▶** *Item stashed.* He stares at his empty hand. The clearest crack yet that the System is **real**, not all in his head — and nobody else saw. (Held back; the [climax's decisive tool](system.md), introduced quietly here.)
4. **▶ Earth gear, hidden.** Later, alone, he tests it: stows his last Earth possessions in the pocket where no [torc-scan](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) reaches. The one safe place he owns. (And the surest sign something genuinely crossed over with him.)
5. **A glimpse of the watchers.** On the way out, one beat of the antagonist made almost-visible: a too-still man, a too-clean exchange, the word "drive" — the [data theft](cast.md#antagonist-position) brushing the edge of frame. Lex doesn't notice. The reader does.
6. **The closing image.** Dawn over the [glowing translucent walkways](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/index.md); Milo, mended, grey-torc'd, employed, half-fed, indebted, lied-into and lying, standing at the top of the gradient with a job he can do and a job he shouldn't take. **▶** A clean, smug full [State Card](system-state-card.md) — the foothold quantified — under which nothing is actually secure. Hold on the gap between the card and the truth. **End of Act I.**

*Plants:* running pitched; the spaceless inventory (real, held back); Earth gear hidden; the watchers. *Threads:* all four engines now live and converging; the System (anomaly confirmed to the reader). *State Card:* Torc PROVISIONED(grey) / job / debts / Stash: Earth gear / Quest [MAIN]: stay legitimate-enough; [SIDE]: the wrongness in the data. *↪ Hinge into Act II:* he takes the first run — **therefore** "[Roped In](outline.md#act-ii--roped-in)."
