# The System — the "Hood" interface

The single rule that keeps this from becoming an actual LitRPG: **the System is not real, and it is never confirmed to be**. There is [no magic on Arrhenos and no game engine running the world](../../setting/technology/enhancement.md) — only biotech and physics that Milo can't parse. The HUD is something his own mind produces, a coping artefact that renders an unsurvivable amount of strangeness into the one symbolic language he trusts. It earns its place by *working*; it unsettles by working a little too well. Hold that ambiguity for the length of the book.

## What it is, diegetically

A man drops into total sensory and conceptual overload — [the Shambles at arrival pitch](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) — with a brain shaped by a lifetime of games and a present too painful and too illegible to face head-on. So the brain does what brains under threat do: it dissociates productively. It overlays a familiar interface, turns dread into a quest log, turns strangers into stat-blocks, turns a wound into a debuff. The gamification is **adaptive dissociation** — it lets him act when panic would otherwise freeze him.

Three readings stay simultaneously available **to the reader and to Milo**, and the text should not resolve which is true for a long time. But behind the veil there *is* an answer — **something real crossed over**, his arrival was [not random](protagonist.md), and the anomalies are genuine clues, not all artefacts of a coping mind. (Locked: the series is a genuine SF mystery; see the [bible](bible.md).) The craft task is to keep the deniability *honest* — every clue must also admit a mundane reading — while the truth accumulates underneath:

- **Psychological.** Mostly his nervous system's UI for feelings and guesses he couldn't otherwise hold. True of *most* of the System — which is what makes the real anomalies hide so well.
- **Technological.** Something crossed with him — his own [pattern-reading](protagonist.md) faculty meeting this era's ambient fields, his old [phone](cast.md), his neural wiring. His grey [torc integrates "seamlessly" with the HUD](outline.md): suggestive, deniable, and a real data-point.
- **Stranger.** Whatever *pulled* him here left a residue, and may still be reaching. Keep it faint and mostly off-page in Book 1 — but it is the true floor under the other two.

## How it behaves (the rules of the misreading)

The System is **wrong in consistent, characterful ways**. It is not omniscient; it is *Milo-shaped*. Its readings are his subconscious's best guess dressed as fact, which is exactly why they're useful and exactly why they're dangerous.

- **It interprets, it doesn't know.** It labels a kindly vendor "NPC: Merchant (Friendly)" and a dangerous favour "Side Quest (Reward!)". When it's right, it feels like magic; when it's wrong, the gap is the scene.
- **It gamifies real systems.** The [laggy interpreter routine](../../worlds/arrhenos/culture.md) becomes a *Xeno-Linguistics* skill that levels by listening. Scavenging parts in [the Industrial District](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/industrial-district.md) becomes *Looting*. [Routine medical repair](../../setting/technology/enhancement.md) becomes *healing*. The mechanic is always a real Arrhene thing seen through a gamer's astigmatism.
- **It rewards engagement, not avoidance.** Skills level by *doing*, which keeps nudging Milo toward the world rather than away from it — the System's quiet, accidental therapy.
- **The romance breaks it.** Affinity scores are where the misreading hurts. Reducing a person to a number Milo can grind is the habit the book exists to dismantle; the moments the System fails to capture a feeling are the moments that matter most. See [premise.md](premise.md).

## Mechanics

Keep the crunch light, legible, and in service of character. Every mechanic should map to a real [setting](setting-brief.md) thing.

### Class

**Hood** — a pun on HUD, born the instant Milo yanks his hood up to hide his [bare, torc-less neck](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md). It self-describes as a rogue/underworld archetype, which is just the System rationalising his actual predicament: locked out of [legitimate, torc-gated services](outline.md), he has no option but improvisation, cover, and the grey economy. The class is the disadvantage wearing a costume.

### Attributes

A compact, classic spread — enough to track growth, not so much it becomes bookkeeping. A workable five (final set is the writer's to lock in the [State Card](system-state-card.md)):

| Attribute | Governs |
|-----------|---------|
| **Body** | Physical condition, stamina, the [casual physical robustness of locals](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/index.md) he keeps measuring himself against |
| **Wits** | Quick thinking, improvisation, reading a situation |
| **Lore** | Accumulated knowledge of this world — history, custom, the ~1,800 lost years |
| **Nerve** | Composure under pressure; holding the cover when it's tested |
| **Charm** | Rapport, persuasion, the social game he was hopeless at on Earth |

### Skills (level by doing)

Proficiencies that rise through use, e.g. *Xeno-Linguistics* (listening to locals), *Looting / Salvage* (scavenging), *Streetwise* (navigating the Shambles' [impossible gradient](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md)), *Cover Story* (sustaining the Drifter persona), *Appraisal* (knowing what a thing is worth — sharply relevant to the [pawn](cast.md)). Add skills as the story generates them; don't front-load a tree.

### Loot and rarity

Scavenged and acquired objects are rendered as loot with rarity colours — a comic, useful misreading of an [economy that runs on the quid (ϟ)](../../setting/trade-and-currency.md) and on what a thing will actually fetch. The joke has teeth when the System's rarity rating and the real [market value](cast.md) disagree.

### The spaceless inventory

The clearest **real** anomaly: Milo can stash objects in a pocket the System calls his inventory, and uses it to hide his [Earth gear](outline.md) where no torc-scan can reach. It is the sharpest crack in the "it's all in his head" reading — deploy it *sparingly*, never explain it, and make it **earn its keep at the [climax](outline.md)**, where vanishing the hot data-drive beyond any search is what saves everyone. A capability this large, used this rarely, reads as a loaded gun rather than a convenience; keep it that way. In a world where [everything legitimate is logged](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md), an untraceable pocket is also a quiet, dangerous edge.

### The translated competence

Milo's [systems and pattern literacy](protagonist.md) — worthless on Earth, decisive here. The System frames it as a passive trait or a read-out (route lines, patrol rhythms, an objective marker over the weak point in a flow), but it is really just *him*, turned up. It earns his honest living at the [board job](outline.md) and makes him [valuable to the runners](cast.md); whether the System *amplifies* it or merely *renders* it is part of the [open mystery](#what-it-is-diegetically). Keep such "translated abilities" rare, grounded, and tied to who he already was.

### Levels and XP

Light. A level is a felt milestone — competence consolidating — not a power spike. Treat XP as the System's way of telling Milo he's adapting, which is true whether or not the number is.

## Display convention

System output renders as **blue boxes** — set off from prose in blockquotes or code blocks so the reader sees the interface as interface. Keep them terse, deadpan, and visually distinct from the warm register of the surrounding scene; the contrast *is* the style. The exact recurring template lives in [system-state-card.md](system-state-card.md).
