# Direction — operating instructions for writing *Hood*

This is the file to open when actually writing an instalment. It assembles the rest of the pallet into a repeatable loop. The short version: **read the bible, write the double exposure, update the bible, append the card.**

## Before each instalment

1. **Read the [bible](bible.md).** It is the live state — open threads, loaded plants, where everyone is and how they feel, the timeline, the tonal contract. Writing without reading it is how continuity breaks.
2. **Glance at the [last State Card](system-state-card.md).** It tells you the in-fiction now: time, place, money, torc status, affinities, active quests.
3. **Know where you are on the [arc](outline.md).** Which act, which thread is due development, what the reader is currently waiting for.

## While writing

4. **Write the double exposure.** Every scene is two scenes — the [SF reality](setting-brief.md) the locals inhabit and Milo's [gamified misreading](system.md) of it. Hold both; the gap is the point. Don't let the gamification flatten a feeling.
5. **Render System output as blue boxes.** Set [System notifications](system.md) off from the prose in blockquotes or code blocks — terse, deadpan, visually distinct from the warm human register around them. The contrast *is* the style.
6. **Keep the registers apart.** Cold and clipped for the numbers; close and specific for the people. When they collide, the [human register wins](premise.md).
7. **Honour the setting.** Run the [setting-brief checklist](setting-brief.md): pronouns, torc, 20-hour clock, quid, family specificity, stakes grounded in [setting pressures not imported prejudice](setting-brief.md), romance as default.
8. **Apply the [craft](craft.md).** Reincorporate before inventing; advance by *therefore/but*; keep the [cast small](cast.md); develop a thread rather than opening a new one unless there's reason.
9. **Mind the secret and the System.** The [Drifter cover](protagonist.md) and the [pawned phone](cast.md) raise the cost of every deepening bond; the [romance](premise.md) is the lever loosening the System's grip. Keep both pressures live.

## After each instalment

10. **Update the [bible](bible.md).** Move threads (opened / developed / closed), note new plants, advance the timeline, adjust relationships and the state of the secret, log any setting detail you *added* (so it can be promoted to canon later). Reconcile it with the State Card — they must agree.
11. **Append the [State Card](system-state-card.md).** Update every field that changed, only those. Preserve any divergence between the System's reading and the human truth — don't "correct" an affinity score that's high while trust is actually breaking; that divergence is the story.

## Standing reminders

- **The System is never confirmed real.** Hold the [three readings](system.md) — psychological, technological, stranger — open for the length of the book. Resolve the *human* question (does Milo let himself be known?), not the metaphysical one.
- **Sympathy, not mockery.** The gamified mind is a [trauma response that happens to work](system.md); write it with warmth. Comedy at the world's strangeness, yes; cruelty toward Milo's coping, no.
- **Canon is upstream.** When the story needs a setting fact, [link and obey](setting-brief.md); when the story *adds* one, note it in the bible rather than contradicting the vault. If a genuine conflict with canon appears, flag it for the owner — don't paper over it.
- **Constraint is the engine.** A man who can't read the world, can't prove who he is, and can't tell the truth. Solve problems from *inside* that box; it's where the story is.
