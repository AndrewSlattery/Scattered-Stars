# The System State Card

Append this block to the **end of every instalment**. It is the reader-facing dashboard and the writer's running save-file — the place the narrative and game state are pinned so nothing drifts between turns. It is the public face of the private [bible](bible.md): the State Card shows what Milo's System "knows"; the bible holds what the *author* knows (plants, secrets, the truth behind the numbers).

Keep it **canon-consistent** in its units even though Milo's narration mistranslates everything: time runs on the [20-hour day and the Arrhene month names](../../setting/calendar.md), money is the [quid (ϟ)](../../setting/trade-and-currency.md), enhancement is the [EX scale](../../setting/technology/enhancement.md), and the [torc](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) status is a live stat because it gates everything.

## Field notes

- **Time.** Use the [Arrhene months](../../setting/calendar.md) (Daw, Thall, Kerannym, Acros, Nivir, Dimanate, Vorago, Oscur, Edrise) and the 20-hour clock — *midday is 10:00, midnight is 20:00*. Milo may privately gripe that the clock is "wrong"; the card stays in local time.
- **Torc.** Three states — **NONE** (arrival; Social Stealth Disadvantage, locked out of legitimate services), **Placeholder** (the medic's stopgap; passes casual scans, fails real ones), **Provisioned** (the real one bought with Lex, [HUD-integrated](outline.md)). Always note the live social effect.
- **Cover.** A standalone gauge because the [Drifter persona](protagonist.md) is load-bearing and degradable; pair it conceptually with the *Cover Story* skill.
- **Cred / Debt.** Money in [quid (ϟ)](../../setting/trade-and-currency.md). Debt tracks the [pawn against the Earth phone](cast.md) and any money owed to Lex — keep them visible; they are the plot's ticking clocks.
- **Attributes.** The five from [system.md](system.md). Lock the final set once and keep it stable.
- **Relationships.** Affinity 0–100 with a status word (Wary → Intrigued → Friendly → Close → …). The numbers are the System's reductive guess, not the truth — the [romance](premise.md) regularly contradicts them, and that contradiction is the point. **[Devan](cast.md#devan--the-love-interest) is the one to watch** — his card number will read *colder* than the bond actually is; track it alongside, in the [bible](bible.md), how close [Milo's origin secret](outline.md) is to breaking (on Arrhenos, [secrecy is the betrayal that counts](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md)). Track [Lex](cast.md#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) too — a real friendship Milo is also lying to.
- **Quests.** One **MAIN** through-line (get legitimate / get the phone back / decide whether to stay), plus live **SIDE** threads. Update statuses honestly — let quests fail, stall, and mutate.
- **Inventory.** Three tiers: **Equipped**, **Bag**, and **Stash** (the [spaceless inventory](system.md) — where the contraband Earth gear hides from torc-scans).

## Discipline

Update *every* field that changed, and only those; a card that never moves is a dead card. The State Card and the [bible](bible.md) must agree — reconcile them each turn as part of the [per-instalment loop](direction.md). When the System's reading and the human reality diverge (an affinity score that's high while trust is actually breaking), don't fix the number — **that divergence is the story**, and the card should preserve it for the reader to feel.
