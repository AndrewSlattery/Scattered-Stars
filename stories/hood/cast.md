# Cast

Two disciplines govern this list. **Keep the cast small** — a [constraint that breeds depth](craft.md), and one the lonely-crowd theme actively rewards. And **reincorporate before you invent**: the [Shambles already has documented regulars](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md), and a named character who recurs is worth ten who appear once. Before adding anyone new, check whether someone who already exists could do the job.

## Principals

### Lex (Alexios Makris-Webb) — the reckless friend who ropes him in

**Canon character** ([Westslope Gardens](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md)). Nineteen; eldest son of the warm, prosperous [Makris-Webb household](#the-makris-webb-household); "testing everything," contemptuous of provincial Brovdingonai next to Landwick media, and — the load-bearing canon detail — **building something secret in the building's workshop**. He is [a year short of citizenship](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md), "already half gone into adulthood," and "the younger two are a responsibility he has not yet noticed he accepts." That last line is his whole arc.

Lex is the first local to treat Milo as a person — and, being nineteen, also the first to see him as an *opportunity*. Bored, clever, hungry to matter beyond a comfortable home, Lex runs grey errands for the thrill and the Landwick-cool of it and builds illicit kit in the workshop; when he clocks Milo as a **torcless, recordless, untraceable off-worlder** who is also unnervingly *sharp* (Milo reads the room and calls a thing before it happens), he sees the perfect deniable accomplice. Not a cold handler grooming a patsy — a reckless kid pulling a vulnerable stranger into something he himself only half understands, sure he can control it. His generosity (a [grey torc](outline.md), a stake, a floor to sleep on) is genuine *and* angled, and he doesn't notice the difference until it's dangerous.

- **Function.** Milo's door into the city's [underside](#the-runner-network) and into an [ordinary Arrhene family](#the-makris-webb-household); the hand that ropes him into the [intrigue](outline.md). A friend, a foil, and a catalyst — **not** the love interest (that's [Devan](#devan--the-love-interest)).
- **The arc — recklessness to responsibility.** The kid who hasn't noticed he's responsible for anyone gets everyone he loves endangered by his own cleverness, and at the [climax](outline.md) finally *steps up* — spends himself, not someone else, to protect Milo and his brothers. He grows up a year early, the hard way.
- **The relationships to track** (see [bible](bible.md)): his affinity with Milo (a real, deepening friendship, complicated by the fact that Milo is lying to him too); his guilt as the danger dawns; and his bond with his brothers [Corin](#the-makris-webb-household) and [Nikias](#the-makris-webb-household), the stakes that grow teeth.

### Devan — the love interest

An adult (early-to-mid 30s) [Shambles](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) fixture: he runs a legitimate front — a parcel, repair and salvage desk in the lower levels — that quietly doubles as a node in the [runner network](#the-runner-network). He has spent years keeping his head down and his profile low, for reasons of his own that echo Milo's predicament more than Milo realises; it's why he reads Milo so fast, and why he distrusts what [Lex](#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) is doing. They meet because Devan tries to **warn Milo off** — an off-worlder with no torc and no record is exactly the kind of person who gets spent in this world, and Devan has seen it before.

- **Function.** The book's emotional spine and its warmth against the [System's cold numbers](system.md). Where Lex pulls Milo toward the grey thrill, Devan is the steadier pull toward a real life worth choosing — and, being himself a man who straddles the legit and the grey, the person who can actually walk the dangerous work beside him. The romance grows the [canon way](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md): **shared competence**, body-first ease, interest signalled in understatement (grand gestures "read as provincial").
- **The central tension — secrecy as the only betrayal that counts.** On Arrhenos [love is not rivalrous](../../worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md); what ends households is not other lovers but *secrecy, neglect, a broken promise*. Milo's hidden origin is therefore precisely the kind of thing that breaks an Arrhene bond — so the closer he and Devan get, the more the lie about *who he is* becomes the one wound that could undo them. This, not jealousy, is the romance's engine, and it stays unresolved past Book 1 (the [secret holds](outline.md)).
- **The arc.** Wary protector → reluctant partner → the man Milo most wants to tell the truth and most cannot. Endangered when the [intrigue](outline.md) spills over, which converts the romance from subplot to stake.
- **Texture to settle (writer's call).** What Devan is hiding and why he went quiet; his exact footing between clean and grey; his surname (he mostly goes by just *Devan*, which suits a man staying low). Give him his own want and his own secret, so the lying runs both ways and the eventual reckoning is mutual. Log specifics in the [bible](bible.md) and the [additions to promote](bible.md#additions-to-promote-to-canon).

### The Makris-Webb household

[Lex's](#lex-alexios-makris-webb--the-reckless-friend-who-ropes-him-in) family ([canon](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/westslope-gardens.md)), and the ordinary Arrhene life Milo gets a glimpse of at [lunch](outline.md) — comfortable, intellectual, non-rivalrous, extending "beyond blood and legal ties." It is also what's in danger when Lex's recklessness goes wrong, which is why the family doubles as plot: two of the fathers work the very data the [antagonist](#antagonist-position) is after.

| Member | Canon | Story use |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Suren Makris** (37) | A father; **quantum predictive modelling for agricultural lending** | His professional world *is* the antagonist's target — a lending-model irregularity at his work is an Act-I plant and the family's thread into danger |
| **Elio Webb** (39) | A father; **medical data & neural-interface coordination**; proud of his roast dinners | His neural-interface expertise quietly touches the [torc/HUD](system.md) mystery; the cook who feeds Milo the most disorienting meal of his life |
| **Darien Foss** | Suren & Elio's lover of six years; agricultural systems monitoring; family | A third adult in the home; another set of eyes on the agritech data; takes Nikias to football |
| **Corin** (16) | Quiet, watchful, journals; "the likeliest confidant" | The brother who *notices* — a risk and a possible ally; sees more of Milo than is safe |
| **Nikias** (11) | Opinionated; wants to be a jockey; at the [Placement](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md) threshold | The youngest stake; his hero-worship of Lex sharpens the climax's danger |

### Sorian — the broker who knows too much

An antiques-and-curios broker, upmarket of the Shambles, to whom Lex steers Milo expecting easy quid against the **Earth phone**. It doesn't go to plan. Sorian deals in [pre-Scattering artefacts](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md) and therefore knows, at a glance, that this one is *wrong*: not merely old but **impossibly** intact, quietly anomalous, the kind of object that shouldn't exist. So he doesn't hand over a fortune — he advances a modest sum and **keeps the phone**, on strings, because curiosity (and the smell of profit, or worse) won't let him pass it on. Milo walks out only half-solvent, and a shrewd, [connected](#the-runner-network) man now holds the most dangerous object on the planet and a set of questions about it.

- **Function.** A slow-burn threat that **converges with the intrigue** rather than solving the money problem. The phone is a Chekhov's gun with a battery; Sorian's questions are the fuse. His curiosity draws the same dangerous attention the [runner network](#the-runner-network) has drawn, which is how the two threads meet at the [climax](outline.md) — where the phone is finally spent, taken, or destroyed, with Sorian left a loose, ticking end for Book 2.
- **What he does *not* work out.** That Milo is a time-displaced Earthman. Sorian concludes the *object* is anomalous and valuable; he doesn't reach the man. Keep it that way — the [origin secret holds](outline.md).
- **Debt as leverage.** The pawn is the legibility theme made concrete: Milo's freedom mortgaged to the one artefact that could expose him, in the hands of a man who already half-suspects it.

### The medic — first contact at the desk

The medical attendant on shift at the Shambles clinic/inquiry desk where Milo first surfaces. Patches up [old injuries with routine biotech](../../setting/technology/enhancement.md) (an early, quiet marvel — the future is casually kind to his body in a way Earth never was), fits the **placeholder torc**, and points him upmarket to a tech shop and a clothing chain. A small but pivotal role: the first human face of the new world, and the person whose questions force Milo to commit to the Drifter cover on the spot.

## Supporting and recurring

### The bar group

The men Lex is drinking with when Milo falls in. Milo cadges food from genuine need, settles in to listen, and harvests social grounding — [family](../../worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md), work, life in Brovdingonai and Arrhenos generally — while fielding idle questions about the Drift ("tin-can homes, and *more than one gender*?"). A reusable ensemble: name two or three, let them recur, and let one matter later far more than he seems to — when the [intrigue](outline.md) puts friends in danger, these are some of the friends. [Reincorporate](craft.md), don't invent new victims.

### The runner network

The grey courier world Lex belongs to: men who move what the [torc-logged official channels](../../worlds/arrhenos/society.md) can't or won't, hand to hand and roof to roof, exploiting the seams of a city where [everything legitimate leaves a record](../../worlds/arrhenos/government.md) and physical delivery is the only untraceable kind. Milo's legitimate [board job](outline.md) gives him the flow-map the runners covet; his [pattern-literacy](system.md) makes him frighteningly good at reading their routes and the gaps in surveillance. Somewhere above the runners is whoever the **hot data-drive** belongs to — the Book 1 antagonist faction, **deliberately left open** (see below).

|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Venn Sarko** | Mid-range barber, no appointments | The shop is a low-grade information exchange; the natural place for Milo to overhear, or be overheard about. A bare neck is exactly what a barber notices. |

## Antagonist position

Two layers, and the [setting forbids the easy villains](setting-brief.md) (no homophobia, no prison-yard brutes — keep it that way):

- **Structural and internal**, all book: the **bare-neck problem**, the **debt and the phone**, the **cover that must not crack**, and the **System itself** — gift, crutch, or cage.
- **The human faction (chosen, and woven):** an **agritech-lending data-theft operation**. Brovdingonai sits in the [agritech belt](../../worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/industrial-district.md), and Arrhenos runs on [financial services](../../worlds/arrhenos/economy.md); stolen predictive-lending models and the **hot data-drive** that carries them are worth killing for. Behind the local grey buyers is a [Sable Cartel](../../powers/sable-cartel/index.md) intelligence shadow (**Blackwell**), present in the deniable, omniscient-by-reputation way the Cartel is everywhere and nowhere — never confirmed, always plausible. This is *woven*, not bolted on:
  - **The family.** [Suren Makris](#the-makris-webb-household) models exactly this lending data; the irregularity that unsettles him at work is the operation's footprint, and it puts his household in the blast radius.
  - **The board job.** [Milo's](protagonist.md) dispatch desk gives him a god's-eye view of the same flows; his [pattern-brain](system.md) notices the theft's signature before he understands it.
  - **Sorian.** As an [information-broker node](#sorian--the-broker-who-knows-too-much), he sits in the buyers' ecosystem; his fascination with the anomalous phone draws the operation's eye toward Milo.
  - **Lex.** The kid is [couriering](#the-runner-network) for this without grasping whose it is.
  - **The origin-resonance.** Let the drive's predictive-model / [neural-interface](#the-makris-webb-household) contents brush, once and unexplained, against *how Milo was pulled here* — the thread that lets Book 1's intrigue and the [series mystery](outline.md) eventually prove to be one thing.
- **Still open** (pick to set Book 2's lean): the **visible face** of the operation — a smooth local fixer/buyer, ideally [reincorporated](craft.md) from someone already on the page — and *how directly* the Cartel shadow is ever shown. Track the choice in the [bible](bible.md).
