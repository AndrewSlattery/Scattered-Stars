# Union of Frontier Freeholds: Locations

The Freeholds span hundreds of systems, and the map is never finished: settlements rise, bust, merge and splinter faster than anyone charts them.

| Korsen's Anchorage | The frontier's marketplace and neutral ground; seat of the Cartel's Nexus       |
| Veritas Station    | Mercenary and intelligence hub; the Veritas Compact, and the Cartel's Aegis     |
| Dustmote           | The Freehold whose people remade themselves unable to leave                     |
| Roughneck          | A boom grown into a working industrial world, owning class and all              |
| Three Falls        | The frontier at peak rush; an aurite strike with corporations closing in        |
| The Sefkir Reach   | A dozen Freeholds that expelled the Cartel in 3829 C                            |

## Regional groupings

The Carina Reach is seventeen Freeholds bound by the Carina Compact into the Union's most integrated bloc—mutual defence, standardised trade, joint exercises. Its major settlements are Carina Prime, Harrow Station (the current Chair's home) and the Foundry orbital.

The Trailing Border holds the systems pressed against Mandate space: contested claims, military tension, heavy militia investment, and a border that turns a Freehold system into an Imperial one a few years at a time. Major settlements are Redoubt, home of the Freeborn Brigade; Farsight Station; and the Marchward settlements.

The Salvage Reaches are thick with pre-Cascade debris and dead colonies. Salvage there is active, competitive and occasionally violent, with minimal permanent population. Major operations run at the Cassini Shoals, Wreckfall, and the Dormant Fleet coordinates.

The Deep Frontier holds the furthest settlements—minimal infrastructure, high risk and reward, effectively ungoverned even by Freehold standards. New Providence is a failed terraforming reclamation; Ultima Station is the furthest permanent human settlement in this direction.

## Settlement types

Communities sort less by geography than by where they sit in the cycle of boom and decay.

| Boom towns          | Temporary concentrations around a strike—Three Falls for aurite, Mirren's Deep for organics, the Shoals camps for salvage |
| Bust towns          | What remains when a boom ends: Golconda, Esperance, the Shimmer settlements                                              |
| Stable Freeholds    | The rare ones that found a durable base—Roughneck, Dustmote, the Carina settlements                                      |
| Corporate towns     | Settlements owned and run by corporations, with company governance and workers who have limited political voice. There are many, and most are unnamed |
| Stations and orbitals | Constructed habitats: trade hubs, processing complexes, military posts, and refuges for those who prefer a built world to a born one |

## Navigation and claims

Frontier space is poorly mapped, by neglect and on purpose both: sparse jump-shell infrastructure, unreliable beacons, deliberately uncharted systems, and route knowledge so valuable it is itself traded. Pilots who work Freehold space develop local lore outsiders lack and guard it as the professional asset it is.

Territorial claims overlap, conflict and shift. There is no central registry, a claim is held by whoever can defend it, and disputed ground may be claimed by many parties or none. Onto all of this the Mandate lays its own border claims, so that a system the Empire calls Imperial may contain Freeholders who emphatically disagree.
