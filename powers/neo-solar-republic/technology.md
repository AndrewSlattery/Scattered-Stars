# Neo-Solar Republic: Technology

What other polities call experimental, the Republic calls routine. None of it is evenly spread—most citizens use a fraction of what the Republic can do—but the frontier is real and the Republic sits alone on it.

## The Lattice

A quantum-neural mesh of entangled processors, relay nodes and implanted interfaces that knits individual minds into a persistent shared awareness.

| Neural interfaces  | implanted cognitive hardware linking a mind to the mesh                    |
| Relay nodes        | distributed processing and communication infrastructure                    |
| Entanglement cores | quantum-entangled processors for instantaneous local coordination          |
| Archive substrates | long-term storage for cognitive patterns, memories and citizen backups     |

True shared consciousness needs proximity. Within Sol and its near orbits the Lattice is instantaneous; beyond it, the mesh falls to light speed, and a citizen far from home is, for the first time in their life, alone in their own skull. Outposts run local Lattice clusters that synchronise with the Synthesis core by courier, never in real time.

Citizens raised on the Lattice find its absence a kind of sensory deprivation, and long separation brings real cognitive distress. This is why so few of them travel, and why the Republic's diplomats are a small and slightly haunted caste.

The mesh is also the single richest target in human space—a successful strike on the core could fracture the collective mind—so the Republic pours resources into keeping it whole.

## Morphology

Citizens commission custom bodies from the Morphological Gallery on Synthesis: forms for zero gravity, deep ocean, hard vacuum, pure aesthetics, or purposes with no functional description at all. Most citizens never go remotely so far.

Moving a mind between bodies is routine—pattern extraction, integrity check, instantiation in the new substrate, continuity confirmation—taking hours for a simple transfer and longer for the hard crossings, biological to digital or single to distributed. A citizen who has changed bodies a dozen times will still tell you which change cost them something they had not expected to lose.

Since the 3850 Census a citizen may legally copy themselves into independent forks, each a true instance of the same base identity, each diverging from the instant of the split. Re-merging is possible and grows harder as the forks accumulate separate lives. Forking solves the problem of wanting to be in two places and creates every other problem in its stead: which fork speaks for the original, which inherits, which is loved, which is liable. The law has answers. The families, often, do not.

## Experimental physics

The Republic's physicists hold that jump-gate operation can be driven by quantum field manipulation rather than fez catalysis, and in 3861 published a proof of concept: a three per cent efficiency gain, with no fez, under laboratory conditions. Three per cent is operationally nothing, and the framework behind it implies far larger gains are reachable. If the Republic ever achieves fez-independent gates, the galactic economy reorders overnight.

Energy comes from compact high-output fusion reactors powering individual habitats, advanced orbital solar arrays for baseline power, and experimental harvesting of energy from vacuum fluctuations. Beneath all of it sit the most advanced computational substrates in human space, the hardware the Lattice and the digital citizens are made of.

## Export policy

| Medical enhancement (EX 1–3)         | widely sold; a major export                                        |
| Basic neural implants                | sold; the foundation of many polities' comms infrastructure        |
| Lattice-compatible neural implants   | restricted to approved partners                                    |
| Morphological technology             | heavily restricted; to Collective researchers by agreement         |
| Computational substrates             | export-controlled; small quantities at premium prices              |
| Lattice core technology              | never, under any circumstances                                     |
| Experimental physics                 | classified                                                         |

Every polity wants the tier above the one it has, which keeps the export schedule a permanent diplomatic sore.
