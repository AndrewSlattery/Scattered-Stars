# Citizens and Subjects

Citizens possess voting rights in local and regional assemblies, full freedom of movement, access to all professions and education, and rights including protection against arbitrary detention. By contrast, subjects require travel permits, are barred from certain professions, cannot vote, and are vulnerable to procedural abuses.

Proportions of the two classes vary wildly from system to system and city to city. Citizenship passes by lineage; mixed parentage produces subjects unless the citizen parent sponsors elevation.

There are several paths to citizenship, including a decade or more in the military, entrance to the civil service, recognition for exceptional contribution by the powers that be, and monetary purchase.

## The Uncounted

Some people defy the binary, in the Substrate beneath Hiveholm, along the ragged edged of the Provisional Territories, or between the cracks of any city large enough to get lost in. Life without a legal existence affords certain freedoms but also invites official and unofficial dangers.
