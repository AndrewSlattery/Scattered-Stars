# The Mandate

The Mandate defines itself by harmony and hierarchy. It is the largest unitary political entity in human space, and its gestures at uniformity belie great diversity.

The prestigious Mandate Classical is the second language learnt by people speaking hundreds of regional tongues. Although the Mandate's people are categorised into the two boxes of "citizens" and "subjects", that whole apparatus is invisible throughout the lives of the vast majority.

The Mandate has a population of circa 2.4 trillion people, and it is ruled from the throne world of Tiamming. Its on-paper absolute imperial autocracy is in practice a vast bureaucracy that can more easily be steered than commanded. The current sovereign, 17 Xuanzong, has spent 41 years on the Stellar Throne and at 96 has not named a successor.

The military of the Mandate is the largest in human space; a portion of it is presently occupied in blockading the Yansieve rebellion.

## Citizenship

Not everyone in Mandate space is a citizens of the Mandate. The remainder are "subjects", whose movement, professions and legal rights are somewhat circumscribed. The impact of this distinct in daily life can be negligible (as in the agricultural hinterlands of Tiamming or off-the-grid warrens anywhere), or it can define every interaction (as in the most explotative industrial areas or the least carefree noble estates).

## Current Events

A dozen or so frontier systems have been in open revolt (the Yansieve rebellion) since 3858 C, governing themselves through a porous imperial blockade. They demand more autonomy and the abolotion of the citizen-subject distinction.

A suspicious number of imperial ships are deployed to Kepler-442, their purpose and status classified.

## Misrule

Each Gap 1 the emperor ceremonially abdicates and formal hierarchies invert until sunset on Gap 3, when the emperor is crowned anew. It may only be theatre, but it is a society-wide performance.
