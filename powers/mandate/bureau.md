# The Bureau of Internal Harmony

The Bureau of Internal Harmony (colloquially "the Bureau") is the genuinely formidable, widely feared, yet quietly overstretched subdirectorate of the Ministry of Harmony responsible for keeping order within the Mandate.

The Bureau monitors threats to imperial stability. "Threats to stability" may be interpreted to include political dissent, organised crime, separatism, corruption, "disharmonious" movements, foreign intelligence, and whatever else the Bureau decides belongs on the list this year.

## Methods

Informant networks operating through coercion, conviction or coin are feeding files of millions of the Mandate's population. In many places there is an unverifiable sense that someone in the room is reporting. In fewer cases the Bureau may directly monitor communications through remote means or through physical infiltration.

There are statistics on the Bureau's arrests and interrogations in relation to identified crimes; there are none on those few who disappear for longer than the prescribed three-day period.

## Structure

The central Bureau is headquartered in the Chrysanthemum Court. Offices covering whole sectors and cheifs posted to individual systems or stations (both with broad local authority) report in regularly. Covert operatives known as the Unseen are unnumbered and embedded everywhere, or at least anywhere.

Lady Fen Aravis, aged seventy-one after twenty-three years in the post, is the current Minister for Internal Harmony. Her reputation is based more on fear than fact, because the few facts that are recorded—every mission a success, every rival a failure—are doubtless little more than a highly and ruthlessly curated narrative. Her own views and loyalties are either pragamtically flexible or truly divided.
