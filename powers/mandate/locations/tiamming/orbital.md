# Tiamming Orbital Infrastructure

The space around Tiamming carries thousands of installations and a system-wide orbital population of some 320 billion.

## The Ascension Gates

Twelve space elevators tie the surface to orbit and carry the bulk of Tiamming's cargo traffic. They are imperial infrastructure operated by the Ministry of Transit, and each has grown its own character over the centuries.

| First Gate         | Chrysanthemum Court | oldest and most prestigious; grand ceremonial terminals |
| Second to Sixth    | Xuan coast          | mixed passenger and cargo                               |
| Seventh Gate       | Hiveholm            | industrial traffic; never stops moving                  |
| Eighth to Eleventh | Parvati coast       | heavy cargo and industrial supply                       |
| Twelfth Gate       | Osthavn             | agricultural exports on a seasonal rhythm               |

Transit takes about four hours from the surface to synchronous orbit, in accommodation running from luxury cabins through standard seating down to cargo pods. The Gates are reliable enough that a breakdown is newsworthy.

## The Jade Belt

Private stations at synchronous altitude where aristocratic families keep residences clear of planetary concerns. No imperial protocols, no petitioners, access by invitation, security private and absolute. The residents are active nobles wanting privacy, retired ministers, and the occasional subject rich enough to buy proximity without ever buying status.

The Floating Gardens, a station kept at low gravity and given over to extravagant hydroponics—water flowing in ribbons, plants growing in impossible directions—hosts the social functions where the powerful meet outside any official setting, and where a good many arrangements are made that appear in no ministerial record.

## High Tiamming

The military complex at L4: around 3 million naval personnel, with a fleet base, shipyards and sensor arrays. Multi-year rotations have bred persistent communities with their own traditions and slang, and career officers who spend more of their lives here than on any surface.

The station is markedly quieter than it was three years ago, with ships and crews pulled off to the trailing frontier, those remaining standing longer rotations, and the rotation cycles returning Yansieve veterans subtly changed.

## Commercial and defence infrastructure

Cargo stations transfer goods between the elevators and interstellar shipping, the largest handling millions of tonnes a day. Shipyards build and repair vessels of every size, and account for a significant fraction of the Mandate's naval and commercial fleet. Sensor networks watch every approach; automated and crewed defence platforms could not stop a determined invasion fleet but would extract a terrible price from one; patrol forces maintain a continuous presence throughout the system.

## Access

Citizens book passage, present credentials and travel. Subjects require a permit, approval depending on purpose, destination, record, and the temper of the reviewing official. The Jade Belt admits by invitation regardless of status. High Tiamming admits military personnel and authorised contractors only.
