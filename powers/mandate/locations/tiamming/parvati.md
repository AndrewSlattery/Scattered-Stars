# Parvati

The equatorial continent of Tiamming, around 7 billion people, where the core's industry lives. An empire of hundreds of worlds builds almost everything locally, but Parvati holds the prestige tier: the fleet's honour-yards, the pattern-forges whose designs the provincial worlds copy, the works against which the rest of the Mandate measures itself.

About sixty-five per cent of the population are subjects, the highest proportion on the throne world. The reasons are structural rather than decreed—industrial work does not require citizenship, comfort-seeking citizens do not move to industrial regions, and status passes down the generations along with the jobs. The citizens who are here cluster in the roles that need legal standing or confer authority: managers, administrators, security.

Shared conditions produce a practical solidarity across the line that the northern estates lack. When the atmospheric scrubbers fail everyone breathes the same bad air; when the transit seizes everyone walks. The distinction still matters, but it is daily and physical rather than abstract and ceremonial. It also stops at the door: the citizen can leave and the subject cannot. Parvati sends more of its subjects into the military than any region in the Mandate, and its veterans come home citizens.

Parvatan dialects are distinct from Xuanese Classical, and code-switching between the tongue of home and the tongue of getting on is constant.

## Locations

Hiveholm, 2.3 billion people in a vertical industrial megacity, is the largest city on Tiamming.

The Extraction Zones in the interior handle mining and resource processing, with harsh conditions, transient workforces, and company towns where the operating corporation simply is the local authority.

The Coastal Manufacturing Belt runs lighter industry along the northern coast, with better conditions than the interior and proximity to shipping.

Port Avalon is the largest spaceport, moving bulk cargo for export. It is less prestigious than the Ascension Gates and handles far more tonnage.

## The Yansieve factor

Parvati follows the rebellion closely, its subjects sharing both legal status and accumulated grievance with the rebels. The Bureau monitors, and the conversations continue more carefully. Few here are ready to rise.
