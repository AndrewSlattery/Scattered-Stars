# Tiamming

The throne world: seat of the Stellar Throne and administrative heart of the Mandate, though not its most populous system. Some 18 billion people live on the planet and around 340 billion system-wide.

Tiamming Primary is a G2V star. The planet is slightly larger than Earth, sixty-eight per cent ocean, at 1.02 standard gravity, with three continents and long island chains. Its climate is managed rather than radically terraformed, so the seasons still turn and the north still has real winters.

| Xuan    | northern                    | ~8 billion | imperial heartland; aristocratic estates      |
| Parvati | equatorial, extending south | ~7 billion | industrial and manufacturing; subject-majority |
| Osthavn | southern                    | ~3 billion | agricultural, provincial                       |

Xuan rules, Parvati makes and Osthavn feeds, and each is convinced it is the part of Tiamming that actually matters.

Around three in five of the planetary population are citizens. The throne world runs citizen-heavy, as core worlds do; out on the industrial and peripheral worlds the ratio inverts.

In orbit are twelve space elevators (the Ascension Gates), the aristocracy's private stations in the Jade Belt, the L4 military complex at High Tiamming, and a thick weather of commercial stations, shipyards and defence platforms.
