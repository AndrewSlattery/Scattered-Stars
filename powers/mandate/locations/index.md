# The Mandate: Locations

The Mandate spans hundreds of systems. Documented here are the throne world and a handful of places that matter to the present moment.

| Tiamming             | Throne world and administrative heart, covered continent by continent    |
| Marag                | A middling agricultural province, weeks off the trunk routes             |
| The Yansieve systems | Trailing frontier cluster in revolt since 3858 C, self-governing under blockade |
| Kepler-442           | Active military deployment; purpose and status classified                |

Hundreds of other systems—older colonies, sector capitals, industrial and agricultural worlds—go undocumented here, and Marag stands in for most of them.
