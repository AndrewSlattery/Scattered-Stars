# Lyra

The throne world of the Vega Commercial Throne: around 4 billion people at 0.94 gravity, in the Vega system.

Vega's star is fierce, and Lyra's climate has been managed for millennia—orbital mirrors moderating the light, processors holding the air, weather scheduled rather than suffered. Visitors from harder worlds find it unsettling that nothing here is raw.

## Aurelian

The northern continent, where the aristocracy lives, landscaped into a single enormous stage: rolling parkland, manicured forests, elegant manor houses, and supporting infrastructure tucked discreetly out of frame. Each archducal house keeps its primary seat here, and the estates are operational centres as much as residences.

## Meridine

The equatorial continent, where the work happens: urban centres, commercial districts, and the professional and labouring classes who serve aristocratic enterprise without belonging to it. Its capital, Aurelius City, holds around 800 million people and was never meant to be glamorous—good transit, adequate services, real employment, comfortable lives lived without aristocratic pretension. It is also where the fallen land.

The continent holds the Exchange, Lyra's commercial trading hall, a shadow of what it was before Kraeven but adequate to domestic commerce. Meridine is most of Lyra's actual life, and appears in almost none of Lyra's stories.

## Australis

The southern continent feeds the others and keeps the old ways: agriculture, seasonal rhythm, community, minor nobility and prosperous commoners, traditional values, distance from court fashion.

Its Vintners' Valleys produce the prestige wines poured at court, and its vintner families occupy a peculiar rung—not noble but respected, not rich by archducal standards but comfortable, connected to power through what power drinks. Dame Vesper Holt, the most famous of them, has dined with the Queen and would rather be in her vineyards.

The young leave Australis for Aurelius City and the orbitals. Those who stay generally prefer the quieter pace, and the freedom of not being watched.

## Orbit

Lyra's orbital space carries the Court's ceremonial annex, commercial and industrial platforms, defence installations, and the great production orbitals of Stellavista and the Manufactories. The rings are visible from the surface.
