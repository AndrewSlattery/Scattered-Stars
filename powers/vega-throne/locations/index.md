# The Vega Commercial Throne: Locations

The Throne spans several systems, but its heart is the Vega system, and the heart of that is one world.

## The Vega system

| Lyra              | Throne world; the aristocracy on Aurelian, the working population everywhere else |
| The Radiant Court | The royal palace complex on Aurelian                                              |
| Stellavista       | The entertainment orbital, where the content is made                              |
| The Manufactories | The orbital where luxury goods are made                                           |
| Outer stations    | Mining, agriculture, and the support that keeps the system running                |

## Beyond Vega

| Deneb Reach            | Agricultural worlds; food production; where many an aspirant comes from |
| Altair Cluster         | Manufacturing; middle-market goods                                      |
| The Andromedan Holdings| Disputed frontier; the Banking War's unhealed edge                      |
| Outward stations       | Trading posts and distribution hubs                                     |

## Fiefdom geography

Noble fiefdoms are distributed by prestige, which is to say by distance from the Throne. The Aurelian continent of Lyra holds the archducal seats; Vega orbital holds the station-based commercial houses; Deneb Reach holds the older agricultural nobility; and the frontier holds the newer houses and recent grants, where opportunity is real and status is thin. A house's address is a statement of its standing.
