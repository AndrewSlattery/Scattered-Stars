# The Manufactories

An orbital complex trailing Stellavista by forty degrees around Lyra, some 8 million strong at a standardised 0.85 G, where Vega produces what "Vega-made" signifies.

## The four zones

The Design Ring is where concepts become products and a successful designer can achieve near-celebrity: house studios, independent designers, materials libraries, prototyping.

The Artisan Quarter is where traditional craft persists, in workshops producing bespoke work by hand, because scarcity is the luxury and the labour itself is what the customer pays for.

The Production Halls run scalable manufacturing—automated and semi-automated lines turning out licensed goods that are still better than the alternatives, just not unique.

The Showrooms are where products meet buyers in curated, flattering spaces, the selling begun before a price is named.

## Authenticity

Authenticity is policed by embedded markers, database registration and a certification regime pursued with near-religious ferocity. A counterfeit that passes does not steal a sale, it dissolves the meaning the entire sector floats on, which is why the houses cooperate instantly and absolutely on brand protection.

## The two kinds of worker

The artisan is trained for years, narrowly specialised, and produces limited work that he signs, so that individual identity attaches to the product and genuine prestige to the person. A master craftsman may not be rich by aristocratic standards, and he can refuse a commission, which is how everyone knows he is somebody.

The production worker is adequately paid, regularly scheduled and largely interchangeable, with little prospect of advancement, making luxuries he cannot afford for customers he will never meet. The work is not harsh and it is not fulfilling either.

The guilds—Tailors', Jewellers', Furniture, Leatherworkers'—set training standards, certify credentials, and preserve craft knowledge individual houses might let lapse. Their authority stops where the houses' control of production begins.

## Places

The Cassiline Atelier is where Vega fashion still originates, collections debuting here before they reach the markets that will imitate them. The Gilded Vault holds House Therenne's reserves of gold, platinum and gemstones. The Certification Hall is the gate between production and market. The Archive preserves centuries of design for the designers who mine the past to make the present.

## People

Master Artisan Kell Soren, a jeweller of sixty years' reputation, has settings collectors seek by name, and has declined noble commissions that failed to interest him. "The hand learns what the mind cannot teach. You must do the work ten thousand times before you understand it."

Designer Lyric Vane, five years out of the academy and signed to Cassiline, is a nostalgic modernist by the critics' label she dislikes and accepts for its marketing value. "They love me now. They'll love someone else later. What matters is the work while I can do it."

Quality Director Tam Essien, senior official of the Certification Hall, has rejected products from every great house, and his judgement is final: a commoner with an absolute veto over the work of nobles, tolerated because the system's honesty depends on someone who cannot be charmed. "If I approved substandard work, the system collapses. Everyone knows this. They still resent me for it." He buys little, owns less, and lives in a small apartment far from the luxury he guards.
