# Stellavista

The entertainment orbital above Lyra: some 12 million people across a complex of studios, residences and distribution infrastructure, at gravities from 0.3 to 1.0 G depending on the section.

## The machine

Concentric Production Rings house the major studios at varying gravities—the Outer Ring for Radiant's prestige drama and Eventide's immersive work, the Middle for Cascade's music and the smaller houses, the Inner for royal Starfire and the technical facilities.

The Talent Quarter is where performers live, in residences graded from a star's luxury to an aspirant's shared dormitory. Living there is privilege and constraint at once, close to the work and close to the observation.

The Processing Centres finish nearly all Vega content—editing, effects, sound, quality control—and the Distribution Hub is where it leaves for human space. The Hub is the chokepoint, and House Valdorian's grip on it, along with its studios, is what makes the house's dominance more than a matter of market share.

## The hierarchy

| Stars                 | luxury residences, personal staff, every comfort |
| Established talent    | good housing; choice of work                     |
| Working professionals | adequate housing; steady employment              |
| Aspirants             | shared quarters; ceaseless competition           |
| Support staff         | variable; the crews who build it all             |

The hierarchy is not abstract here. Different levels eat in different cafeterias, residential areas are separated, and social mixing is limited and noticed.

Young hopefuls arrive constantly, from across Vega space and beyond, carrying training and talent and savings into a competition almost all of them will lose. Housing is dear, work is scarce, the savings drain, and the years pass without a breakthrough. The predictable costs—substance use, mental-health collapse, the exploitation desperate people accept—are known and not prioritised.

## People

Vex Calder is a former star two decades past his fame, taking supporting parts and teaching master classes, managing a smaller apartment with care and no visible bitterness. "The industry takes what it needs and discards the rest. I gave good years; I received good payment. Complaining would be unseemly."

Liora Thrace, an aspirant from Deneb Reach three years in, has nearly exhausted her savings, shares a dormitory with three others, and is still auditioning. "Ask me again next year. Either I'll have broken through, or I'll have gone home. Either way, I'll know."

Director Drusa Verro is one of Radiant's senior directors, with thirty years of prestige productions, a recognisable style, no interest in celebrity, and modest habits by Stellavista standards. She reinvests her earnings into work she believes in. "The work is the point. The rest is noise."

## Where they meet

The Promenade is the public entertainment district and the station's real marketplace of careers, where opportunities surface in conversation as often as through any formal process. Studio 7, where *The Amber Throne* has filmed for sixty-seven seasons, is an unremarkable room made sacred by association and included reverently on the tours. The Audition Halls process thousands of hopefuls a day through a liturgy of waiting, brief performance and vague response. The Observation Deck, with its view of Lyra turning below, is where industry people go to talk without being overheard.
