# The Vega Commercial Throne: Houses

A Vega house is a business that happens to be a family, and it is judged every quarter. The rank is real and hereditary, and it can be taken away for losing money. Around three and a half thousand noble families sit at the top of a population of one and a half trillion.

## The churn

The Banking War was the great reshuffling, and the standings still carry its marks.

The winners compounded their luck into dominance. Valdorian bought its rivals' wreckage and rose; Meridian's reliability became priceless when nothing else could be trusted; Corvane supplied the reconstruction.

The losers are still paying. Kraeven collapsed and was dissolved; Cassiline was gutted and recovers by inches; Aurelian drifts down the rankings, a craft house in an age that has stopped buying craft.

Therenne and the royal house itself simply held, which in a market this brutal is its own achievement.

The result is a court less comfortable than a generation ago. The houses that profited resent being resented, the houses that suffered resent the profiteers, and all of them watch Valdorian grow.

## What a house is

A great house is a conglomerate wearing a coronet. Beneath the Lord or Lady and the designated heir sit a family council, the house offices that run the administration, the commercial enterprises that generate the revenue everything depends on, a private retinue under arms, and a class of retainers—the non-noble staff whose proximity to nobility is itself a kind of status, families serving the same house across generations.

The aristocratic and the commercial are not two faces of the house. Status is bought with revenue and spent on the display that secures more revenue.
