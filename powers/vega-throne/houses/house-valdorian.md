# House Valdorian

The dominant entertainment house, ascendant since the Banking War. Archducal rank, around 500,000 direct employees, some 150 billion quid a year in revenue, and roughly a third of all Vega entertainment production—a concentration without precedent in living memory.

## The rise

Elevated to nobility in 3412 C for early success in entertainment, the house climbed on a single repeated move: quality production, strategic acquisition, relentless expansion. Baronial to comital within a century, ducal by 3600 C, archducal after the consolidation of 3702 C.

The Banking War was the masterstroke. It damaged Valdorian but devastated its rivals, and when the shooting stopped Valdorian had carefully preserved capital while its competitors had only debt. The house bought struggling studios, the distribution networks of failing rivals, and the talent contracts bankrupt employers could no longer honour.

## Duke Caelum Valdorian

Ninety-four, presenting mid-forties, head of the house for thirty-one years since 3831 C. He is the architect of its wartime survival and post-war dominance: the man who preserved capital while rivals spent, and bought the distressed assets at the bottom.

He is not liked. Rivals find him predatory and his own people find him distant, and he has never seemed to mind either judgement. In thirty-one years he has never once moved against the Throne, and the loyalty is performed with the same cold precision as everything else he does.

The Duke has no children. His designated heir is his nephew, Lord Evander Valdorian, 67, who runs the music division: competent, not brilliant, and untested at the scale his uncle operates. Factions within the house already favour other candidates the Duke declines to discuss. His sister, Lady Virellia Valdorian, has handled the delicate work of absorbing acquired studios and the resentments that come with them, and is the more approachable of the two and the more underestimated. "Caelum sees numbers. I see people. Someone has to." Whether she wants the chair, nobody knows.

## Holdings

Radiant Productions, the flagship studio, defines prestige drama; *The Amber Throne* has filmed there for sixty-seven seasons. Cascade Entertainment runs music from development to touring under Lord Evander. Eventide chases the growing immersive sector. Valdorian Lifestyle turns entertainment into fashion-adjacent merchandise and, lately, into the premium-fashion market, where it grinds against a wounded Cassiline.

The house seats at Starhold on Lyra's Aurelian continent, an estate half residence and half showcase, built like a small fortress. It holds substantial portions of Stellavista, where its control of production facilities and the distribution hub becomes leverage over every rival who must use them.

## Relations

Meridian cooperates, news serving entertainment marketing. Cassiline is openly hostile: Valdorian took its talent during the war and now circles what is left. Therenne stays neutral, Corvane commercial, and Aurelian is dismissed as a fading thing.

When the royal succession after Caspian finally comes due, the house best placed to exploit an uncertain Throne is also the house the rest of the aristocracy would most like to see stumble.
