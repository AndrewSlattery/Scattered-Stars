# Haven Ascendant: Preservation

The Haven's twin pillars: the Archives, which hold the record, and the Reconstruction Halls, which let you walk into it.

## The Archives

The holdings span pre-Scattering material from the original *Mnemosynë* collection—physical artefacts, digital records, genetic samples, sensory recordings; Cascade documentation from the 847 worlds that declared independence in 2756 C, much of it preserved nowhere else; the archives of lost worlds since failed, destroyed or gone dark; Drift chronicles, oral histories and logs; and the deposits entrusted by individuals, families, corporations and governments who wanted something kept.

In total: 2.4 exabytes of digital material, 40 million physical artefacts, and 847 environmental reconstructions on file.

| Public     | general history, entertainment archives                | free                    |
| Registered | academic materials, detailed cultural records          | nominal fee             |
| Restricted | sensitive deposits, legal records, identity archives   | negotiated              |
| Sealed     | materials held in trust under specific conditions      | by terms of deposit     |

The Haven's entire value rests on one boast it has never broken: sealed material stays sealed. Deposits have waited centuries for their trigger conditions and none has been opened early.

## The Chronicle Service

For a fee, the Haven will document anything—a birth, a death, an agreement, a confession, a secret—under whatever access restrictions the depositor names.

For the stateless it is closer to a lifeline than a clerical sideline. A Drift-born child entered in the Haven's record has proof of existence that no coup, no border closure and no failed colony can erase, and a refugee can lodge testimony that may matter long after the people who would suppress it are gone.

## The Reconstruction Halls

A reconstruction is not an exhibit but an environment: a volume of the station rebuilt, as exactly as the records allow, into a place that existed elsewhere or elsewhen. You enter it, breathe air scented as the original was scented, walk surfaces textured as the original was textured, and meet performers trained to inhabit it as its first people did.

| The Paulista District    | São Paulo, Earth                        | 22nd century        | 0.8 km² urban streetscape        |
| The Hanging Market       | Covenant Station, destroyed 2891 C      | pre-destruction     | 0.3 km² market complex           |
| Firstfall                | the Cascade declaration site            | 2756 C              | a single plaza, a single moment  |
| The Yennara Coast        | Yennara, a failed colony                | 2845–2847 C         | 1.2 km coastline, seasonal cycles|
| The Jade Emperor's Court | a reconstruction of a reconstruction    | Tang Dynasty aesthetic | 0.4 km² palace complex        |
| Memorial Corridor        | composite                               | various             | a processional of destroyed places|

Firstfall holds eighteen minutes of the morning of 3.2/1, 2756 C: the crowd gathering in Covenant Station's plaza, the rising tension, the reading of the Vandemeyer Declaration that triggered the Cascade, the first cheers, and then the loop resets.

The Paulista District, the oldest, recreates an ordinary São Paulo neighbourhood nobody important ever came from, documented only because one Foundation researcher grew up there and recorded every detail before he left. Unlike Firstfall it lives: weather, day and night, seasons, and performers who age in their roles and are replaced and age again.

The Hanging Market is a memorial rather than an entertainment, rebuilding a Trade Haven destroyed in 2891 C in a piracy incident that ran past anyone's intent. Its performers are descendants of the survivors, its admission is free, and a visit is understood as an act of witness.

## Confidence ratings

| Verified     | extensive primary sources; high fidelity; citable in scholarship |
| Interpolated | solid framework with reasoned extrapolation                      |
| Evocative    | limited sources; captures the spirit rather than the detail      |
| Speculative  | minimal documentation; labelled plainly as interpretation        |

A reconstruction runs from source research through environmental design, fabrication, sensory calibration and cultural preparation to external academic verification. It takes five to fifteen years and hundreds of millions of quid, funded by diaspora communities, academic institutions, wealthy patrons, reinvested entertainment revenue, and the occasional planetary government.

The Haven would rather mark a hall Speculative and disappoint a paying patron than let a guess pass as a memory.

## The schism

A haven built to preserve what humanity was sits uneasily with a Drift increasingly tempted to become something else. The spread of Neo-Solar enhancement lands hardest here of anywhere in the fleet, because Ascendant's identity is the keeping of the human record, and you cannot easily archive a way of being while your own children vote to leave it behind. The Haven has taken no official position, and the argument runs through the Stacks and the Convocation in lowered voices.
