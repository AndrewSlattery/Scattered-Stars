# Haven Ascendant

A jump-capable Grand Haven of around 4.2 million permanent residents, founded 2691 C, 23 kilometres along its primary axis and some 890 million tonnes. Its Captain-Archivist is Teshen Briar, in post since 3847 C. It specialises in archives, environmental reconstruction and entertainment, and speaks Trade Tongue with Ascendant Drift as its local tongue.

## Origins

The station began as the *Mnemosynë*, commissioned in 2691 C by the Terran Heritage Foundation to preserve Earth's cultures as humanity scattered. The Cascade of 2756 C destroyed the Foundation and the political order that funded it, leaving the *Mnemosynë* in transit between systems with no owner and no mission. The crew voted on whether to seek absorption into some planetary polity or carry on alone, and chose alone, 342 to 7.

What saved them was the cargo. The archives held centuries of Earth performance—music, theatre, dance, games, food—and the crew began selling heritage experiences to passing traders: a reconstructed meal, a recovered game, a play nobody had staged in a hundred years. The novelty drew visitors and the quality brought them back, and by 2800 C the renamed Haven Ascendant had locked its two halves into a single engine, entertainment revenue funding preservation and preserved material generating new entertainment.

The first environmental reconstruction, the Paulista District, opened in 2934 C after eleven years and very nearly bankrupted the Haven a second time. It also defined it. Others preserved records and others sold a good night out; nobody else let you walk through the past.

## The five zones

The Haven is an accretion—the original *Mnemosynë*, salvaged hulls, and purpose-built modules grafted on across eleven hundred years.

| The Core                | original *Mnemosynë*; administration and residence   | ~400,000  |
| The Stacks              | archive storage, research, data processing           | ~200,000  |
| The Reconstruction Halls| environmental recreations, immersive experience      | ~150,000  |
| The Promenade           | entertainment, hospitality, commerce                 | ~800,000  |
| The Outring             | docks, industry, transient housing                   | ~2.6 million |

The Outring—docks, recycling, fabrication, and the churning transient housing of dockworkers, contract crews and the just-arrived—holds nearly two in three of everyone aboard, and almost none of the fame.

Gravity sits at 0.85 G through the residential and entertainment sections, and drops to 0.4 G in the Stacks to spare the physical archives; some Reconstruction Halls retune it to match whatever they are recreating. Threading the whole length is the Axis, a zero-gravity transit corridor whose capsules cross between zones in minutes: public, free, perpetually crammed, and the one place every deck of the Haven physically meets.
