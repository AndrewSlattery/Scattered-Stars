# Story Seeds — live tensions in the setting

*A writer's index: the wound springs the lore currently holds, with pointers. Arrhene material first, since that is where the fiction lives. This file records what is unresolved; resolving any of it is a story's job, not the lore's.*

## Arrhenos: at home

- **Did anyone go further?** The Consortium's models surely flagged Kraeven's instruments years early; Arrhenos reduced exposure in good time. Did Arrhene houses position to profit from a collapse they saw coming and said nothing about? "There is no evidence that they did. There is no evidence that they did not." — [banking-war.md](worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md). A young analyst finding the wrong ledger is a whole novel.
- **The generation gap over the war profits.** The men who lived it defend it without flinching; the younger generation carries "a distaste, never quite resolved, for a fortune made on the wholesale ruin of others." — [banking-war.md](worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md)
- **The Cartel client.** The Consortium banks, watches, and privately blames the arsonist it can never accuse; its surveillance of Cartel flows is itself a quiet export. What a monitoring officer sees, and what he is permitted to do about it — [external-relations.md](worlds/arrhenos/external-relations.md), [sable-cartel/relations.md](powers/sable-cartel/relations.md)
- **The breaks in the neutrality.** A handful of officers leaked, froze, or warned during the war, "out of conscience or out of a bribe," names still spoken "with contempt or with quiet sympathy depending on the speaker." One of them, decades on — [banking-war.md](worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md)
- **The placement that isn't.** The Third Republic abolished the childhood assessment three centuries ago because what it measured was the household a boy came from — and the households still tell, which every generation argues about again. Nikias Makris-Webb intends to be a jockey, his coaches see a runner, and nothing exists to settle it but dinner-table attrition — [fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md](worlds/arrhenos/fatherhood-and-brotherhood.md), [society.md](worlds/arrhenos/society.md)
- **The exclusivist born into a non-exclusive world** — "or its opposite—makes for a familiar kind of heartbreak." — [intimacy.md](worlds/arrhenos/intimacy.md)
- **The bare-necked.** Men who won't wear the torc, managing "with some daily friction" on a planet whose infrastructure assumes it — [society.md](worlds/arrhenos/society.md)
- **The reactionary fringe** — men who find the mixed-gender galaxy contaminating; a cranky minority that knows it is one, and feeds the planet's worst stereotype every time it travels — [women-and-gender.md](worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md)
- **The upland dialects near Zespol** kept a gendered pronoun, used "in old verse and, mischievously, for off-worlders"; the linguists have argued about why for two centuries — [culture.md](worlds/arrhenos/culture.md)
- **The Anvil.** No formal aristocracy exists; eight centuries of informal one does. What it costs to be let in, or to walk out — [campottoni.md](worlds/arrhenos/locations/campottoni.md)

## Arrhenos: abroad

- **Maru at twenty.** The diaspora's second generation is welcome in law and celebrated in principle; the practice is a girl raised in two grammars deciding whether to visit a planet where she'd be, for most purposes, the only woman in the province — or a port-born son standing with a Foundation Day cohort that has never met anyone like him — [diaspora.md](worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md), [little-arrhenos.md](worlds/arrhenos/locations/little-arrhenos.md)
- **Coming home with a family.** Wives and daughters are as welcome as any woman on Arrhenos — the doors are open; the demographics are the wall. A household weighing return against the reality of so few other women around, long-term — [diaspora.md](worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md)
- **The arbitration tribunals sit until 3890** — a whole career spent settling other people's war, in Vega, among an aristocracy that cannot forgive being rescued — [banking-war.md](worlds/arrhenos/banking-war.md), [little-arrhenos.md](worlds/arrhenos/locations/little-arrhenos.md)
- **The cousinly correspondence.** Arrhene scholars and Resheph's have exchanged letters for two centuries. Send someone — [women-and-gender.md](worlds/arrhenos/women-and-gender.md), [minor-powers.md](powers/minor-powers.md)
- **Vethallen.** Roughly a third of a crew stays aboard with the homeworld right there below. The *Polletio* is ninety-seven years old and full of men who never quite land — [polletio.md](worlds/arrhenos/vessels/polletio.md), [diaspora.md](worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md)
- **The falsely accused misogynist.** An accusation "harder to answer than to disprove, since the disproof is a shrug" — a diaspora man's reputation, a hostile negotiation, a shrug that costs him — [diaspora.md](worlds/arrhenos/diaspora.md)

## The powers: successions and binds

- **The Mandate's empty throne.** Xuanzong XVII, 96, no heir; crush/contain/concede; every ministry gaming the vacancy; Fen Aravis preparing for several successions at once — [mandate/government.md](powers/mandate/government.md), [bureau.md](powers/mandate/bureau.md)
- **Vega's empty cradle.** Caspian, 89, childless heir; every archducal house quietly gaming the generation after next — [vega-throne/government.md](powers/vega-throne/government.md)
- **Valdorian's untested succession.** Whether the dominance was the house's or merely Caelum's; Lady Virellia, "the more underestimated," who may or may not want the chair — [house-valdorian.md](powers/vega-throne/houses/house-valdorian.md)
- **Meridian's line.** How closely can the trusted news house cover the house with the private army? "A credibility that bends, even once, even quietly, is a credibility that has begun to spend the only capital the house owns." — [house-meridian.md](powers/vega-throne/houses/house-meridian.md)
- **Cassiline's vice.** Isadora vs. Lucan, quality vs. volume, and Valdorian circling; the royal wardrobe already diversifying — [house-cassiline.md](powers/vega-throne/houses/house-cassiline.md)

## The powers: slow fuses

- **Yansieve.** Four years of stalemate; the whole empire "quietly doing a sum." The lights are still on down there — [yansieve-rebellion.md](powers/mandate/yansieve-rebellion.md), [yansieve-voices.md](powers/mandate/yansieve-voices.md)
- **The Sefkir example.** "Whether it spreads or fades is, in a real sense, the open question of the Union's whole future"; the Cartel works to keep it unglamorous; Sera Voll, 24, has not decided — [sefkir-reach.md](powers/union-freeholds/locations/sefkir-reach.md)
- **The Census's direction of travel.** Each decade a step toward the Integrationist horizon; a vote that can enlarge personhood can also narrow it — [neo-solar-republic/culture.md](powers/neo-solar-republic/culture.md), [governance.md](powers/neo-solar-republic/governance.md)
- **The Tender franchise.** Solveig pressing, Valtonen holding, "deferred" doing more work each decade; a Tender has never yet named a world — [elysian-collective/governance.md](powers/elysian-collective/governance.md), [culture.md](powers/elysian-collective/culture.md)
- **Gate research vs. the fez economy.** 3% in a laboratory, decades out, and every fez trader already knows; the Tethys Consortium funds rebuttals, the Cartel seeds operatives — [neo-solar-republic/technology.md](powers/neo-solar-republic/technology.md), [setting/technology/fez.md](setting/technology/fez.md)
- **Roughneck's general strike.** Jace Holloway Sr. planning "something larger"; his son organising Three Falls; Darrow Vance hardening — [roughneck.md](powers/union-freeholds/locations/roughneck.md), [three-falls.md](powers/union-freeholds/locations/three-falls.md)
- **Three Falls will bust.** Wren Aldis is free to leave and cannot go; the corporations are already winning; "in two years it will be booming still, or declining, or a ghost" — [three-falls.md](powers/union-freeholds/locations/three-falls.md)
- **Dustmote's reversal research.** "Staying should be a choice, not a sentence" — liberation or betrayal, decades out, if possible at all — [dustmote.md](powers/union-freeholds/locations/dustmote.md)
- **The enhancement schism in the Drift.** Settled haven by haven, because no authority exists; hardest aboard the archive that keeps what humans *were* — [drift-communities/overview.md](powers/drift-communities/overview.md), [preservation.md](powers/drift-communities/haven-ascendant/preservation.md)
- **The veterans' arithmetic on Marag.** The lodges follow the Yansieve dispatches "with the specific attention of someone checking a sum"; the empire trained, armed, and disillusioned them itself — [marag.md](powers/mandate/locations/marag.md), [mandate/military.md](powers/mandate/military.md)
- **Tomas Ru's annotations.** A Solano schoolteacher quietly correcting the company textbook against the archive box in the cellar; a filing far above has noted the requisition, without alarm, in the way such systems note everything. "Almost certainly, nothing will come of it." — [solano.md](powers/sable-cartel/solano.md)
- **A bloom on Tethys-Var.** Five million workers arriving on no notice, wildcats racing a decay curve on unlicensed ground, an Arrhene crew on enforced downtime in the roughest port in settled space — and every fortune on the moon a bet that fez stays necessary — [tethys-var.md](worlds/tethys-var.md), [polletio.md](worlds/arrhenos/vessels/polletio.md)
- **The Abrantine Terraces.** Ascendant reconstructing a Mandate pleasure complex destroyed at Yansieve, over objections, while the rebellion burns; Vell Kiruna's answer is three words — [characters.md](powers/drift-communities/haven-ascendant/characters.md)

## The mysteries (keep unsolved)

- **The Empty Quarter Event** (3644 C) — eleven systems, forty million people, infrastructure intact and unattended; the Drift won't schedule stops and doesn't say why — [appendices/mysteries.md](appendices/mysteries.md)
- **Kepler-442** — a classified deployment the Mandate keeps supplied through a war it is losing patience for; the system is, on every published account, worthless — [appendices/mysteries.md](appendices/mysteries.md)
- **The Lazarus Anomalies** — terraforming in no known style, older than reliable records; Solveig's teams now carry detectors everywhere — [lazarus-project.md](powers/elysian-collective/lazarus-project.md)

*The three could be arranged into a story; the arrangement is a minor in-universe literary genre, and every version contradicts the others. Canon connects nothing and rules nothing out.*
