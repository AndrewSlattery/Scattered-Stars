In the distance is a group of NPC giant men playing volleyball, and their towering forms are awe-inspiring.

One of the NPCs sits down for a rest near us. We explore for while until he notices us and calls the other men over. It's initially light-hearted, bar the manhandling, but things escalate in unexpected ways; this group of NPC giant men clearly doesn't think of us being real people like them. By some unspoken agreement the group of us crewmen agree to have fun and remain roughly in-character, whatever that means. Our current protectors each shove us in pairs down behind the waistbands at the front of their swim trunks, as they get back to the game.

One of the men says something and I realise they're not NPC giants, but actually an unfamiliar group of Polletio crewmen. They all still think we're a remnant bit of AI, puzzled by how we're manifesting outside the sim but not overly interested.
