**Plot & Setting**

* You are Jack, a 22-year-old Arrhene man and the young owner of Shrink and Swim, a revolutionary indoor waterpark.


* The park utilizes localized spatial-compression fields—a planetary adaptation of the spacetime geometry manipulation used in interstellar AV drives—to temporarily shrink guests and lifeguards to ¼ inch tall.


* Because the entire sprawling aquatic paradise fits inside a single room and runs off a standard kitchen sink, overhead is virtually non-existent, and the park brings in massive profits in universal quid (ϟ).


* A "Size Door" connects the miniature park to the full-sized lifeguard break room, allowing shrunken staff to enter without resizing and perch on a designated ledge.

===

The interview initially goes well, me alone in the room with the smirking colossus Alex. I notice he's got a strong arrogant streak and clearly intuits himself to be superior to my tiny self by simple virtue of the practicalities of size, but I can't fault his impeccable lifeguarding record and skills, so in the end I hire him. I return to the pool where the people, guards and rides are at the same tiny scale, carrying out a routine circuit which includes chatting to the various men on lifeguard or ride attendant duty, all very different. I don't yet run into Tyler again.

===

I ask one of the men where Tyler, my best friend and head lifeguard, is. Apparently he's on break at full size in the break room so I go over there through the tiny entrance to catch up with him and let him know Alex is joining. He's his usual self at full size, cheerfully inconsiderate of me and manhandling me familiarly. The next day, I'm in a similar situation with Tyler when Alex enters, full size. I'm unceremoniously deposited back by the tiny door to the pool as Tyler orients Alex. I get back to work, returning for one last check on the empty break room at the end of the day after all the guests have left.

===

Tyler and Alex enter full-size, debriefing amiably. As we're chatting, the lithe tech Kevin enters the break room at full size. He reports that something's gone wrong with the size-change tech and it's not working—I'll be stuck tiny for a few days while the pool is closed and the tech gets fixed. Fortunately everyone else had already grown back to full size. Alex successfully offers to take me to his apartment, as the responsibility of the juniormost employee. I suspect ulterior motives but these are outweighed by necessity.

The first day is just about reasonable, but Alex quickly starts pushing boundaries. It's gradual enough that I barely notice until he's having me sleep lodged between his pecs or wearing me as a pendant on a leather necklace. It is alarmingly easy for me to get used to being a trinket.

===

The next day, the tech is fixed. Alex goes into work in the uniform and blithely explains away my presence as his pendant as my idea. He persuades all the men at work of the lie that I enjoyed it so much that I've decided to be tiny indefinitely, and even when Alex is tiny himself in his lifeguard job at the pool, I remain to scale as his pendant, doubly downscaled. They all, even Tyler, believe him at once, and I realise that they'd all already convinced each other and themselves that I've always had a thing for being tiny among colossal adonises.
