===

The genie's wish ripples out and through time, making it so that Arrhenos men have always had the ability to sell their size to pay off debts or to buy size at vast expense, changing the entire history of our world. We see the tiers of this retconned Arrhenos; there are size classes across orders of magnitude, some much smaller than I and some that make Rhen and his lot themselves look minuscule.

===

Later that day in the city, Rhen runs afoul of a hundred-footer, a titan-class guard and bouncer, in his arrogance, and gets repossessed. He isn't shrunk but he becomes the property of the guard until the guard deems him reformed, which he never intends to.
