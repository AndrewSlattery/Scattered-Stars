I determine to test this further. Every few minutes I adjust Brad's height another 10cm taller. At first the torc is only retconning Brad and his history. Once he surpasses 2.5m and later 3m, it starts having to go further afield, gradually editing the Polletio to fit him and eventually even editing Arrhenos demography in general to allow for the mundane existence of a separate category of men much taller than usual. All the while the changes are seamless, conversations with crewmates adapting to the circumstances perfectly.

#

Over the coming days, my curiosity won't let me stop. As Brad passes 4m tall, I suddenly notice that a few more of the crew are roughly his height, as though plausibility (the torc's apparent main directive, imposed to pay off any paradox debt) demands that more of his sort of Arrhenos men, culturally exactly like most Arrhene except much taller, be on the crew. Past 5m and the ship is fully bifurcated with different sections for each size class. 

#

Past 7m, I realise with a start that our suave, trim captain himself has always been a giant. Apparently, plausibility demands that the hierarchy concord with height. Past 10m and the remaining small men like me have practically become an underclass on the ship, fit for menial maintenance and repair work.

#

Later, I scan through historical records of Arrhenos again to see how things have escalated. My eyes catch on my incher fathers' records.

#

I discover over the next days how the taller crewmen deal with inchers like me day-to-day. It involves a lot of manhandling and rather humiliating or intimate interactions, especially in the showers and in the barracks, where one of the guys often decides for fun to lodge me somewhere on his person overnight, whether between his pecs, in his armpit, or under his waistband. 

#

I wonder, if I keep making Brad taller, whether plausibility will eventually force me myself to grow and become a true man of Arrhenos. However, as I grow him past 20 and then 30 metres, what happens instead is that we tiny men are increasingly not seen as real people by true Arrhenes, more like playthings or tools, and this increasingly becomes our universal legal position as well.
