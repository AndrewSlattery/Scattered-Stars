I am from a normal-size town, but Magnus Pollet accepts male students from all size backgrounds, from as small as a fraction of an inch to as large as thousands of feet tall.

==

Once I arrive, I look around. I see two athletic giant men glancing arrogantly down at us. I describe the rest of the overwhelming scene. ==

Alternatively, I can choose to be the dorm-mate of a giant man in a giant part of campus. Since I would take up proportionally almost no space in such an arrangement, I wouldn't have to pay any accommodation costs. I weigh the options. [end before I decide]

==

Conscious of my father's extremely limited means, I decide on the giant option. The administrator hands me a map and a room card. I take the normal-size transportation to a giant dorm building and find my assigned room.
