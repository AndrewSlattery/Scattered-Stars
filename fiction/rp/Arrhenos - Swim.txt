Shrink and Swim - Lore Document Setting Overview Shrink and Swim is a revolutionary indoor waterpark on Arrhenos that utilizes advanced shrinking technology to create an exhilarating, immersive experience. The entire park fits within a single indoor room, but for its shrunken guests—who are reduced to just ¼ inch tall—it becomes a sprawling aquatic paradise. Every visitor, including lifeguards, is shrunk upon entry and returns to normal size upon exiting. Shrinking Technology & Rules Guests and staff shrink via specialized portals at the entrance and exit. The effect is temporary and reverses automatically upon leaving. A Size Door within the park connects to the full-sized lifeguard break room, allowing shrunken individuals to enter without resizing. The Waterpark (From a Tiny Perspective) Attractions: Towering slides, a powerful wave pool, a swift lazy river, and miniature snack stations. Water Mechanics: Water is carefully regulated to maintain thrilling yet safe currents. Equipment: Tiny inflatables, swim tubes, and rescue boards are provided for guests and staff. Lifeguards & Safety Measures Lifeguards operate at the same scale as guests to ensure safety. They have specialized rescue tools and a communication system linked to full-sized staff. In emergencies, lifeguards can exit normally or use the Size Door to access the break room while still shrunk. Lifeguard Break Room Located adjacent to the park, it features normal-sized furniture, refreshments, and lockers. Most lifeguards grow back to full size before entering, but some prefer using the Size Door and remaining tiny while perched on a special ledge. I am Jack, a man of Arrhenos. I am the young owner of the Shrink and Swim waterpark. The early afternoon was always the busiest time at the park, with people pouring in for a day of fun. I made my way toward the front desk, ready to do my usual rounds and chat with guests. Being the owner of Shrink and Swim, it was part of my job to make sure everyone felt welcomed. At just 5’5” and fairly thin for a 22-year-old, I knew I didn’t exactly have an intimidating presence, but my enthusiasm made up for it. “Welcome to Shrink and Swim!” I greeted a family of four as they stepped up to the entrance. The two kids were staring in amazement as they shrank down before their eyes, entering what—at full size—looked like nothing more than a knee-high plastic box. Their dads fished through his pockets for money or smiled politely, keeping an eye on his increasingly impatient children. I watched them head inside with a satisfied grin. This place really was something special. I mean, who would have thought of shrinking people to fit into a tiny water park? The best part? The entire water supply ran off nothing more than a kitchen sink! And thanks to the solar-powered shrink ray, costs were low and productivity was high. This place practically prints money, I thought to myself, watching more families excitedly pay their admission before heading toward the rides. After checking in at the front desk, I strolled through the park, inspecting a few of the rides. Tubular Time was always a favorite among families, while thrill-seekers flocked to the Skin Burner, a speed slide that made you feel like you were flying. The wave pool and lazy river were perfect for those who didn’t want to wait in line. Eventually, I found my way to the Employee Only section, where the break room entrance was located—at least, the shrunken entrance. There were two ways in: the normal-sized route, which required walking around the back of the park, or the much easier way—shrinking down and stepping inside. I wonder who's on break right now, I mused, glancing down at my notepad. One of my responsibilities was making sure lifeguards weren’t running late for their shifts, and sometimes I had to step in to relieve them if needed. Flipping through my notepad, I noticed I had a few new employee interviews coming up. Hiring was another part of my job, and I usually conducted interviews while shrunken, in a designated room near the break area. “Huh, looks like we’ve got a few newbies coming in soon,” I muttered to myself, skimming through their profiles. The candidates were all young guys looking to become lifeguards at the park. “There you are, boss!” I looked up to see my head lifeguard, Tyler, jogging toward me. “Oh hey, Ty. What’s up?” I asked as he stopped in front of me. At 6’5” and built like a tank, Tyler was an impressive sight—wavy brown hair tucked under his red lifeguard hat, brown eyes hidden behind sporty sunglasses, and a strong, dependable-looking face. “You seriously don’t remember what you need to be doing right now?” he asked, crossing his arms. I blinked, realizing my eyes had wandered down to his tanned, sculpted abs before quickly snapping back up. “Uh…” I hesitated, racking my brain. Tyler sighed. “Dude, you gotta interview one of the newbies, Alex; he's been waiting in the full-size interview room for ten minutes! I know you like to stay small to interview new guys while they stay full size.” [end before the interview starts.]

The interview initially goes well, me alone in the room with the smirking colossus Alex. I notice he's got a strong arrogant streak and clearly knows himself to be superior to my tiny self, but I can't fault his impeccable lifeguarding record and skills, so in the end I hire him. [end as I return to work and Alex goes home for now]

==

I return to the pool where all the people, guards and rides are at the same tiny scale, carrying out a routine circuit which includes chatting to the various men on lifeguard or ride attendant duty.

==

I ask one of the men where Tyler, my best friend and head lifeguard, is. Apparently he's on break at full size in the break room so I go over there through the tiny entrance (which has me remaining tiny) to catch up with him and let him know Alex is joining. He's his usual self at full size, cheerfully inconsiderate of me and manhandling me familiarly.

==

The next morning, I'm in a similar situation with Tyler when Alex enters, full size. I'm unceremoniously deposited back by the tiny door to the pool as Tyler orients Alex. I get back to work, returning for one last check on the empty break room at tiny size at the end of the day after all the guests have left. [end with me alone and tiny in the break room.

==

Tyler and Alex enter full-size, debriefing amiably. As we're chatting, the athletic tech Kevin enters the break room at full size. He reports that something's gone wrong with the size-change tech and it's not working—I'll be stuck tiny for a few days while the pool is closed and the tech gets fixed. Fortunately everyone else had already grown back to full size. Alex successfully offers to take me to his apartment, as the responsibility of the juniormost employee. I suspect ulterior motives but these are outweighed by necessity.

==

The first day is just about reasonable, but Alex quickly starts pushing boundaries. It's gradual enough that I barely notice until he's having me sleep lodged between his pecs or wearing me as a pendant on a leather necklace. It is alarmingly easy for me to get used to being a trinket.

==

The next day, the tech is fixed. Alex goes into work in the shirtless uniform and blithely explains away my presence as his pendant as my idea. He persuades all the men at work of the lie that I enjoyed it so much that I've decided to be tiny indefinitely, and even when Alex is tiny himself in his lifeguard job at the pool, I remain to scale as his pendant, doubly shrunk. They all, even Tyler, believe him at once, and I realise that they'd all already convinced each other and themselves that I've always had a thing for being tiny among colossal Adonides.

==

A few workdays pass, and the other men—even Tyler eventually—grow used to treating me as objectifyingly as Alex does, taking their cues from him. The evenings at Alex's bring their own onslaught of manhandling and chipping away at any autonomy. At one point my tiny clothes all get accidentally torn apart but it seems too inconvenient to bother replacing them.

==

Alex regularly works out at the gym while wearing me. He meets and starts dating a dark guy from there, a head taller than even Alex at six-ten. The man is very on board with and indeed into the situation with me. Eventually he joins the pool as a new lifeguard.
