Genre: LitRPG / Portal Fantasy / Sci-Fi / M/M Romance
Setting: The Arrhenos universe (specifically the city of Brovdingonai).
The Hook: A man from present-day Earth (2025) is transported to this far-future, all-male sci-fi society. To cope with the trauma and the advanced technology he doesn't understand, his consciousness interprets reality through a sci-fi RPG Interface.
The Conflict: * The World's View: To the locals, his lack of torc makes him obviously an off-worlder. There is no magic, only advanced biology and physics.
The MC's View: He sees health bars, loot rarities, objective markers, and dialogue trees.

Primary Location: Brovdingonai. A chaotic, multi-level market district perfect for hiding.
Societal Norm: Arrhenos is an all-male society. This is the baseline normal.
Romance: The story features M/M romance as a central pillar. The protagonist will encounter men (locals) who are potential allies and romantic interests.

Skill Proficiency: Skills level up by doing (e.g., Skill: Xeno-Linguistics levels up by listening to locals).

Scene 1: The Old World (Earth, 2025)

Milo takes in the sights of the Shambles with little comprehension, noticing all the ways it's different from what he expects. He pulls up the hoodie to hide his bare neck, and eventually finds what seems to be a clinic or police inquiry desk. Whatever tech they have seems to recognise his language, although the interpreter subroutine is laggy and inefficient. The questioning leads him to adopt the persona of a Drift off-worlder who was robbed, even as he inwardly boggles at the revelation that he's on another planet millennia in the future.

Milo gets scanned for medical issues and gets a bunch of old injuries fixed with the tech of this place. Meanwhile, he gets better at using the hood non-verbally and frantically researches the history and context of where he is, what happened in the intervening 1800 years, and what the "Drift" is, to maintain his cover. 

The medical attendant on shift fits him with a placeholder torc and recommends a tech shop and a big-chain clothes retailer further upmarket. Milo inwardly wonders how on Earth he'll get money—quid?—to pay for anything.

===

On his way through the Shambles, via a drink from a public fountain, Milo falls in with a group of men at a bar. He manages to scrounge a round and snacks off them with a little improvised explanation, and settles in to listen to their banter. He picks up social details and family and life in Brovdingonai and Arrhenos generally. He gets idly asked a bit about life in the Drift—tin can homes and more than one gender—and he makes up something apparently plausible. One of the men, Lex, offers to accompany him shopping.

===

The shopping for an outfit and a torc (which integrates seamlessly with his HUD) go well; Milo starts recognising individual words and phrases before the interpreter needs to translate them. Afterward, on a guided stroll, Lex sees Milo's phone, and suggests it might fetch a good sum at an antiques pawn shop. The broker, Sorian, is impressed at the mint condition of the phone, getting it working (although it seems to have been factory reset). Milo gets a lot more money for leaving it in pawn than he expected (he'll need to pay it back with interest to get it back), enough to pay Lex back for the torc and clothes, and enough left over for a few weeks at a cheap, cramped but incredibly hi-tech to Milo doss-house.

===

Milo explores the whole city the next day, taking all three levels of tram, setting foot in the long-range intercity train terminal. He has lunch at Lex's house, has a look at jobs in the afternoon, and goes out clubbing with Lex and some of his older friends for an extended holiday outing in the evening. Milo starts noticing how tall, broad and in shape men are in general here.

Milo starts noticing abilities the System gives him that are abnormal even for his new environment, like a spaceless inventory (he puts his Earth gear in there for safekeeping, among other things); some other capability from his old life has translated into useful and unique know-how for a board job.
