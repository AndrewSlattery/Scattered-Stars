Scene 2 spec: Once there, I have a look. In a hidden submenu I find hundreds of unlabelled sims, and we agree to try one, labelled simply VR-47-D, as a group. We end up spawning in together. The sim initialises and we're standing on a beach. At first I think something's wrong with the display, but then I realise we're barely six inches tall! We explore. In the distance is a group of NPC giant men playing volleyball, and their towering forms are awe-inspiring. The NPC men models are based one on each of us.

==

One of the NPCs sits down for a rest near us. We explore for while until he notices us and calls the other men over. It's initially light-hearted, bar the manhandling, but things escalate in unexpected ways; this group of NPC giant men clearly doesn't think of us being real people like them. By some unspoken agreement the group of us crewmen agree to have fun and remain roughly in-character, whatever that means. Our current protectors each shove us individually down behind the waistbands at the front of their swim trunks, as they get back to the game.

==

One of the men says something and I realise they're not NPC giants, but actually really conscious Polletio crewmen. The shtick of VR-47-D is that each group who enter end up having their conscious experiences bifurcated at two different sizes. [Recall that we're with our corresponding giants, so I'm in Giant-Rixon's trunks.] Our parallels only merge back once we leave. The giant copies haven't realised this.

==

After a few minutes, the group of men head out. Somehow, because we're inside their trunks, when they exit the sim the merger bugs out and we're brought with them! We shift with them to being in our captor's uniform briefs, and he notices at once, bringing us out in his coiled fist. They all still think we're a remnant bit of AI, puzzled by how we're manifesting outside the sim but not overly interested. For the moment we're kept as trinkets, stuffed back in briefs, for lack of any other ideas.

==

The crew find many uses for us at work and play. People get suspicious that we've stuck around for so long but it's mostly academic curiosity.
 
I discover over the next days how the crewmen instinctively to deal with us day-to-day. It involves a lot of manhandling and rather humiliating or intimate interactions, especially in the showers and in the barracks, where one of the guys often decides for fun to lodge me somewhere on his person overnight, whether between his pecs, in his armpit, or under his waistband. I find it increasingly difficult not to immediately obey any direct command from a full-size man, which at some point I deduce has something to do with pheromones. The insidious part is how natural the process of subordinating my will and sense of self to theirs feels from the inside.
