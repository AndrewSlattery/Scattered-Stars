Scene 2 spec: Once there, I have a look. In a hidden submenu I find hundreds of unlabelled sims, and I decide to try a solo one, labelled simply VR-47-D. The sim initialises and I am standing on a beach. At first I think something's wrong with the display, but then I realise I'm barely six inches tall! I explore. In the distance is an NPC man sunbathing obliviously, and his towering form is awe-inspiring. [me 6 in, NPC man 6 ft]

==

I explore the man for a while until he notices me. It's initially light-hearted, bar the manhandling, but things escalate in unexpected ways; this NPC giant man clearly doesn't think of me as being a real person like him. Eventually I realise that he's not an NPC but an unfamiliar Polletio crewman, and I manage to communicate that I'm a man too. He takes that in with a blithely amused chuckle, clearly unrepentant. We speculate that the sim loads men in at random sizes. Then, vibrations herald the arrival of a new guy, several times taller again! [me 6 in, guy #1 6 ft, guy #2 30 ft].

==

Garrick manages to be recognised, and the three of us have fun together for a while. Then. Out of the sea comes a titanic figure, bare and muscled, 200 feet tall! The giant man Brutus is clearly experienced with this sim; he knows everything about it. He smugly addresses us tiny guys, letting us know that only the largest man in the sim controls exit rights—none of us three can leave manually on our own—and also that the unlicensed time dilation can stretch indefinitely so that an hour in reality can be experienced as arbitrarily long periods of time in here, and he intends to make the most of us as his trinkets.

==

He follows through. He wasn't exaggerating with "as long as I damn well please" in the slightest. Among other things, I learn that in this world, due to pheromones, it's proportionally hard to disobey a larger man, and harder still as time passes, doing as they say simply seeming like the natural and unquestionable thing to do. I also learn that decades of being three men's trinkets and them being each other's trinkets, along with the obedience, makes it inevitable that I internalise the idea that I'm not really a real man, more of an object to be used.

==

	Brutus has some sort of breakthrough. It allows him to end the sim while causing our real bodies to remain to-scale compared to him. When the sim ends, instead of waking in the cradle I entered in, I am tiny on Brutus' person. Garrick and Jorah are at relative small sizes on him too. The breakthrough retcons our original sizes from everyone's memories; as far as anyone else on the ship is concerned, we never existed as anything except Brutus's toys, now available for any crewman at Brutus's forbearance, who eagerly take him up on the offer.
