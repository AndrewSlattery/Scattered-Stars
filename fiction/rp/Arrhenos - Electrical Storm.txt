==

Next time the chief calls me, I need to go down to the minimum of 5%—just a few inches tall. While I'm working, some sort of electrical storm takes place, seeming to affect both me and the chief who's supervising. Afterward, I am able to use the resizer as normal to grow back to full size and nothing untoward happens for the rest of the day at mess, phys and rec.

==

I wake in my standard two-man bunk somehow back down to 5%, coming to on my bunkmate's bare chest. It's a few minutes before general reveille, and I try to wake him up without causing an incident. He's baffled but carries me out to bring together our mates to try and strategise.

==

The men all pack into the chief's cramped one-man chamber, waking him, standing room only. After the explanations, some testing eventually reveals that the storm has caused my height to be directly controlled by what height the chief mentally expects me to be, prompting more somewhat farcical testing there and then. I'm set to even tinier sizes and even made a giant twelve-footer before being restored to my normal height. For now it seems the chief has to consciously maintain attention on me or I'll default to 5%.

==

At first I'm full-size almost all the time, only getting shrunk when Varrus has a particular job he wants doing. However, over time, the chief gets more comfortable leaving me small in the care of others, sometimes forgetting to grow me back without significiant prompting, once not for several days.

==

I find it increasingly difficult not to immediately obey any direct command from a full-size man, which at some point I deduce has something to do with pheromones. The insidious part is how natural the process of subordinating my will and sense of self to theirs feels from the inside. Conversely, something I give off gradually appears to make full-size men forget that I'm a real animate person and not a tool. They remember dimly on occasion without concern but soon find it easier to go back to their default outlook. By that point I haven't been larger than 5% for a month.

==

Varrus has a breakthrough allowing him to replicate the conditions of the electrical storm. The crew decides that having more resizable crewmen would be efficient. One of my mates Jorak, originally the tallest and beefiest, volunteers to be shrunk out of mild curiosity and interest in a little temporary fun, oblivious to the long-term effects and blithely assuming he'd stay fully in control and full-size most of the time. Taren is the one whose expectations control Jorak's height. [end that first day, before any side effects]

==

The implications of the technology spread further. An officer suggests using stints at 5% as a disciplinary measure for minor infractions. [POV Shift] I am Joss, a crewman suspected of drinking moonshine. Instead of a hearing I'm offered a couple of weeks at 5% under my superior, which seems like a decent deal and less effort that trying to prove my innocence.

==

The issue that no-one notices is that the forgetting effect means that no-one disciplined in this manner is ever restored; the full-size men simply neglect to get round to ending the disciplinary term. Over time hundreds of crewmen end up at 5% indefinitely.
