# Seven — Under the Frame

*Thall 2/2, night, to Thall 2/3, dawn*

You came round in the walk-in with the green cross over the door, which you did not recognise from the inside because you had come in the other way on a trolley, and which you worked out from the poster with the loose corner.

Four hours gone. Second hour of the morning.

Ilan Reyk was sitting on a stool beside the table with his elbows on his knees, in the manner of a man who has been sitting there a while, and when your eyes tracked he said one word and put a beaker in your hand and made you drink all of it before he would say anything else.

Then:

`— you have not slept for six days.`

"I slept."

`— you have a — [ ] — you are two litres down, your pressure went through the roof and then through the floor, you have burns to both — [ ] — which are dressed and which will scar because you would not lie still for the good treatment, and you have had a bleed in both — [ ] — and one in your ear, which is not usual, and which is why you are here and not in a — [ ] — in a chair somewhere with a blanket.`

He let that sit.

`— also you caught seventy kilos.`

"It missed."

`— four people say it missed. two people say you caught it. one man says it went into the fountain, which it did not, because they have drained the fountain. the municipal report will say the — [ ] — will say it went over the far side into the service void and they will look for it in the morning and they will not find it and it will be somebody's paperwork for a year.` He shrugged, entirely bored. `— i do not care where it went. i have four of you in tonight and one of them put his hand in a fryer for a bet.`

⁂

Then he did the thing that ended your peace of mind for the next four months, and he did it while looking at a readout, sideways, not really talking to you.

`— your field kit says something i do not understand.`

You did not say anything. That is the only thing you did right in that room.

`— it is not a — it is not clinical. your clinical is fine, your clinical is a man who has been awake for six days. it is the — [ ] — the ambient. the kit takes a — a field around the patient, for the — it is looking for implants, for hardware, for anything that will react badly to what i am about to put in you. and yours has a — `

He made a shape with one hand.

`— there is a — [ ] — there is a *volume*. about here.` He indicated a space beside your right hip, in the air, about the size of a large bag. `— which the kit reports as present and reports as nothing. it has mass and it has no — it is not describable, it has thrown four exceptions and the fourth one is a — [ ] — is an internal error, and the kit is nine years old and it is Carnival and i have had it drop out twice tonight already.`

He looked at you.

`— you have no implants.`

"No."

`— have you got anything on you.`

And you looked him in the eye and said, "No."

He shrugged and cleared the reading and started talking about fluid.

⁂

You lay on that table with your palms bandaged and let him get four sentences further and then you said, as flat and bored as you could manage:

"What happens to it? The reading you don't understand."

`— nothing. i clear it. i have cleared it.`

"But it's in the record."

`— it is in the record, yes, it is all in the record, the record is the record —`

"Where does the record go?"

And Ilan Reyk, who had been on shift for fourteen hours and had four men in and no reason on this world to think the question was anything other than a foreigner being foreign about paperwork, told you.

`— it goes up. it goes to — [ ] — to coordination. every walk-in and every kit and all four hospitals on this plateau file to the same — [ ] — and none of them have ever agreed about anything, so there is an office whose entire job is that they should. and what that office does, mostly, is exceptions. a kit throws an internal error, somebody has to say whether it is a broken kit or a broken patient. mostly it is the kit. we have had nine of them out this month.`

"How long?"

`— how long what.`

"Before somebody looks at it."

He laughed, actually laughed, in the exhausted way of a man who has been inside a system his whole working life.

`— it is Carnival. it is a queue. six weeks. eight. you will be — [ ] — you will have forgotten. i have forgotten already.`

⁂

You lay very still on a padded table under a green cross with your hands wrapped and did the arithmetic, and it took about four seconds and it did not need any help from anything.

An anomaly with no category, filed automatically by a machine, into a queue.

Nobody is hunting you. Nobody suspects anything. Nobody in that building had a single thought about you beyond fluids. There is only a line in a list, in a backlog, in an office, waiting its turn — and in six or eight weeks somebody will get to it and open it and go *what's this then*, the way you opened four hundred of them a year, the way you had personally, at three in the morning, in an empty building outside Warrington, picked over other people's anomalies for four years because clearing the exception queue was on your objectives.

You were not being pursued.

You had been *raised as a ticket*.

And somewhere on this plateau there is an office of men whose whole function is to reconcile four medical record systems that have never agreed, and you had eaten a roast dinner in the house of the man who runs it, and he had asked you eleven polite questions in four minutes and none of them had been idle, and he had said `permanent state of catastrophe` and laughed for eight seconds, and he liked you.

⁂

Bram was in the waiting room and had been for four hours.

He was asleep sitting up, which is a thing you had seen a man do on that same bench on your first afternoon, and he came awake all at once when the door went, and stood up too fast, and then stopped about a metre away because he did not know what he was allowed to do with his hands.

You said, "I'm fine."

`— you are not.`

"I'm fine, they're keeping me till the fifth hour and then I'm fine."

And he stood there with his arms half up and his burn dressing peeling at one edge and his shirt ruined and said:

`— you went under it.`

"It was going to land on a kid."

`— you went *under* it.` His voice did something. `— there is no version of that where you are — [ ] — there was nothing you could have done, there was not *time*, you cannot get under seventy kilos in — `

And then he stopped, because his mouth had arrived somewhere before the rest of him had, and you watched a very quick man come right up to the edge of a question and look over it.

He did not ask.

That is what he did, and you have thought about it every week since. He stood in a municipal waiting room at half past two in the morning with a thing standing between you the size of a house, and he looked at your face, and he decided — deliberately, you could see him do it — that whatever it was, you would tell him when you told him.

`— all right,` he said.

And he sat down on the bench and put his head back against the wall and did not go home.

He tried to give you money at the fourth hour. Four thousand two hundred quid, the whole of the last day's float, which he had assumed was lost with the coat and which had turned up in your coat, and which he pushed at you twice and then a third time, angrily, and which you took eleven hundred of and would not take more.

⁂

And Odran Sey came in at four.

You did not understand it at all until he explained it, and then you understood it perfectly and it was the coldest thing that happened that night.

`— you have no next of kin. you are a case number. a case number has one registered — [ ] — one relation, and it is your employer, because that is the only thing on the file. so at the twenty-first hour a system sent a message to sey and halloway because a case number went down in a market and there was nobody else to tell.`

He sat down on the chair opposite, seventy-one years old, on the last night of Carnival, at four in the morning, for an unlicensed assistant he had employed for six days.

`— i am not annoyed,` he said. `— i want that clear. i came because there was nobody else and that is a — [ ] — that is not a criticism of you, it is a fact about the file, and it is the exact fact i told you about on your first morning and you did not hear it, and now you have heard it.`

Then he was quiet for a while.

Then:

`— the box.`

You said, "3831."

`— yes.`

"You knew which one I'd have."

`— i knew which three you would have,` said Odran Sey, `— because they are the three, and i have known about them for nineteen years, and in nineteen years you are the first person to find them in a week.`

The room was extremely quiet. Somewhere out the back a machine was running a cycle.

`— i am not going to talk about it in here. i am not going to talk about it in the office either. when your hands work again, come and find me somewhere else, and bring nothing, and in the meantime you are going to do exactly what i tell you, which is: put them back, do not pull a fourth, and do not say the word to anybody, including the boy who bought you the coat, including the man asleep on that bench.`

He stood up and put his coat on.

`— take a week. i will pay it.`

"You can't pay me for —"

`— i can pay whoever i like out of my own firm, and i have been doing it for forty years, and you are not the first man i have carried and you will not be the — ` and then he stopped, and rubbed his face, and said something the layer rendered flatly and without any of whatever was in it:

`— nineteen years, and a boy off a station finds it on a wednesday. i do not know whether to be — [ ] — go to sleep.`

⁂

They let you out at the fifth hour, at dawn, into a city that had just finished a week of being awake and had gone completely silent.

The Shambles at that hour was a wreck: every plaza ankle-deep in wrappers, the temporary stalls half down, three men in municipal orange working a sweeper at walking pace, the walkways still glowing on a programme nobody had switched off. The drained fountain on the second level had a tape across it. Somewhere above, birdsong from modified species in the rooftop gardens, familiar but not quite right.

Bram walked you as far as the top of the stairs down to the racks and then stood there being stubborn about it until you told him, in the plateau tongue, badly, with the stress in the wrong place, to go home.

He laughed at the stress and went.

⁂

Then you turned round and did not go down to the racks.

You went east instead, through the wreckage, up three flights to where two buildings lean together and somebody glassed over the wedge between them two centuries ago, and you stood outside a door with a shutter half down at twenty past five in the morning until a very short man in his sixties came and opened it, because a man who deals in things that must not be findable keeps early hours and had, obviously, been expecting somebody, though not necessarily you.

Sorian looked at your bandaged hands. He looked at the state of your face.

Then he stood up on the platform behind his own counter, worn into two hollows, and got out the bound book and the stylus that leaves a real mark.

`— you are a hundred and seventy-three days from the ticket,` he said, `— and you have not come about the ticket.`

"No."

`— then say it plainly, because i am old and it is very early.`

And you stood in a low room packed with things that had been made a very long time ago and had been kept, and you thought about a queue in an office six weeks deep, and about a man who made roast potatoes and asked eleven polite questions, and about seventy kilos of hot steel that four people said had missed.

"I need something taken out of a medical record," you said. "And then I need somewhere to put a stove."
