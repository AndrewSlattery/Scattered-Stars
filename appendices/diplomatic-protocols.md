# Appendix B: Diplomatic Protocols

## Contact Procedures

Standard protocols for establishing communication between polities. Details vary by region and power.

## Trade Agreement Templates

Common frameworks for commercial treaties. The Arrhenos Financial Consortium maintains widely-used standard forms.

Formal procedures exist but are inconsistently followed. The Cascade of 2756 C demonstrated the limits of diplomatic norms.

Protocols governing:
