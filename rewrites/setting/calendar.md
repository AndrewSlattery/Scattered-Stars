# The C Calendar

The C Calendar is a standard timekeeping system across human space. Planets that have not been synchronised to the standard year usually maintain parallel local calendars of their own.

| second | base unit      | 0.864 SI seconds |
| minute | 100 seconds    | 86.4 SI seconds  |
| hour   | 50 minutes     | 72 SI minutes    |
| day    | 20 hours       | 1 SI day         |
| week   | 10 days        |                  |
| month  | 4 weeks        |                  |
| year   | 9 months + Gap |                  |

On worlds and stations synchronised to the C day, dawn occurs around the 5th hour, midday at the 10th hour, evening near the 15th hour, and midnight at the 20th hour.

The standard date format is recorded as `year C month.week/day`. For example, 3540 C 4.2/7 designates year 3540, the 4th month, the 2nd week, and the 7th day.

## The Gap

The Gap is a period of five or six days inserted between the final month of the old year and the first month of the new year. These days are formatted as `yyyy C Gap day` (e.g. 3862 C Gap 4).

Regional traditions and superstitions have accumulated around the Gap days. More information on the first three of the below examples can be found in the relevant power's files.

| Gap 1–3 | Mandate      | Misrule
| Gap 2   | Hv Ascendant | Supply Assay
| Gap 4   | Arrhenos     | Foundation Day
| Gap 6   | multiple     | auspicious for risky ventures, contracts, and marriages
