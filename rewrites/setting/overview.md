# The Scattered Stars: Overview

3862 C | Human Diaspora

Beginning in 2488 C, humanity began to expand across the galaxy. Today, the diaspora encompasses over 4,000 inhabited star systems, roughly 10,000 terrestrial worlds, and upwards of 50,000 artificial habitats. Over a million stellar bodies have been surveyed and catalogued.

The fundamental constraints on FTL travel have made a centralized galactic government impossible. There have been no serious attempts at unification since the Cascade of 2756 C, when 847 worlds simultaneously declared independence from their Earth-based metropole authorities within a single year.

## The Great Powers

While thousands of independent nations exist, the geopolitical landscape is dominated by seven Great Powers who have successfully leveraged infrastructure, commerce, or military might to project influence across the light-years:

| The Mandate            | A sprawling, imperial hegemony centred on Tiamming.                 |
| Frontier Freeholds     | A fiercely decentralised coalition of border territories.           |
| Elysian Collective     | Terraforming masters who command high prices for their technnology. |
| Sable Cartel           | A ruthless oligarchy controlling vital FTL infrastructure.          |
| Neo-Solar Republic     | Transhuman utopianists holding the ancestral solar system.          |
| Vega Commercial Throne | A feudal society built on media, culture, and luxury goods.         |
| Drift Communities      | Nomadic, space-station societies without fixed infrastructure.      |
