# FTL: Communication

There is no faster-than-light signalling independent of the AV drive. The fastest way to send information between stars is as cargo on a ship with an AV drive. Autonomous courier ships loiter around infrastructure nodes at the jump shells of major systems, transmitting and receiving data from inner systems in light-speed packets.

Messages may travel directly along well-trafficked lanes or make use of intermediate relay nodes for less-travelled routes. One-way communication times thus vary from hours to weeks, but are never fast enough for real-time conversation between systems.
