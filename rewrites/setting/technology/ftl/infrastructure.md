# FTL: Infrastructure

In principle, a ship can emerge from warp anywhere on the jump shell. In practice, they choose to emerge wherever there is existing infrastructure. Services ships require include updated maps, fuel, cargo transfer and maintenance. These services accumulate at nodes (typically a handful per system, sometimes dozens in heavily trafficked space).

Major commercial nodes (fuel, cargo, refits; housing, entertainment) may house tens of thousands permanently, with transient populations several times larger.

Military installations (sensors, intelligence, resupply) may commingle with these or may be kept deliberately separate.

## Jump Gates

Civilisations with sufficient capital may construct "jump gates" on the jump shell.

Gates can lower engineering and economic limitations by projecting externally sustained warp fields and pre-calculating stable trajectories. These allow ships without their own AV drives to transit between stars. Control of a gate represents immense economic prosperity, military advantage, and political leverage.

The alloy "fez" has material properties which drastically reduce gate construction time, resource requirements, and maintenance costs. This makes gates economically viable for smaller or more remote systems. Access to fez thus affects which systems can afford to participate in the galactic economy; see Fez for details.
