# The Sol System

Humanity's origin system, now a complex mix of pilgrimage site, industrial hub, and radical experiment.
## Earth

Governed by the United Nations—one of the few surviving pre-Scattering political structures.
Earth serves as a destination for many types of pilgrimages:

- Historical tourism
- Ancestral research
- Spiritual journeys
- Academic study

The homeworld's symbolic importance far exceeds its political or economic weight in the modern era.
## Mars

Terraformed into an industrial hub. Mars represents successful planetary engineering on a massive scale, though its character has become thoroughly industrial rather than preserving any frontier romanticism.
## Synthesis

The Neo-Solar Republic's capital station occupies the Sol-Earth L5 Lagrange point. See [Neo-Solar Republic](../powers/neo-solar-republic.md) for details.
## Significance

Sol's importance is largely symbolic and historical. The system is not a major economic or political power, but its status as humanity's origin grants it unique cultural weight.

# Tethys-Var

A moon of a gas giant in the Lophore system, and the most important source of fez in human space.

## Overview

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Type | Moon |
| System | Lophore |
| Primary | Gas giant |
| Significance | ~13.5% of galactic fez supply |
## Fez Production

Tethys-Var is the single largest source of [metastable iron-zinc-uranium tennessite](../setting/technology/fez.md), providing approximately 13.5% of the annual harvest.
### The Tethys Consortium

Controls 67% of harvesting licences on the moon. The Consortium's dominance makes it a major player in fez markets, though it faces competition from:

- Sable Cartel proxies
- Arrhenos Merchant Fleet traders
- Independent (often illegal) harvesters
## Population

The permanent population is modest, but millions of temporary workers arrive during unpredictable fez harvests. This transient workforce creates a boom-town atmosphere during harvest seasons.
## Infrastructure

- Dozens of orbital stations
- Surface harvesting facilities
- Processing plants
- Worker housing (temporary and permanent)
## Territorial Disputes

Multiple parties contest control over harvesting rights, processing facilities, and orbital infrastructure. These disputes occasionally escalate to violence.
## Strategic Importance

Control of Tethys-Var means influence over fez markets. Major powers and corporations vie for position through proxies, legal manoeuvring, and occasional direct action.

# Unique Worlds

Worlds with distinctive characteristics that shape their societies in unusual ways.
## Awyr

**System:** Aether

A low-gravity world with a dense atmosphere, allowing for human flight with minimal clothing and equipment.

The combination of reduced gravity and thick air means that with simple wing-suits or even less, humans can achieve sustained flight. This fundamentally shapes Awyr's architecture, transportation, social customs, and sense of space.
## Periapt

**System:** Ptica

A world with such an elliptical orbit that society transforms completely twice a year.

The extreme seasonal variation—between close approach to the star and distant retreat—requires completely different social, economic, and physical arrangements for summer and winter. Periapt's people effectively maintain two parallel civilisations, switching between them as the orbit demands.
