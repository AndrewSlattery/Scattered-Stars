# Lore Roadmap — editorial deep-dive, 2026-07-11

*Follow-up to [HOMOGENEITY-AUDIT.md](HOMOGENEITY-AUDIT.md). Every file in `setting/`, `powers/`, `worlds/`, and `appendices/` has now been read in full. This is the full wishlist: bugs to fix, tweaks, rewrites, expansions, and larger projects, roughly in the order I'd do them.*

**State of the corpus, in one paragraph.** The seven powers are in excellent shape — consistent house voice, real internal variety, live tensions (the three successions, Valdorian, the Tender franchise, the Census, Sefkir). The corpus runs in two registers: rich essay (powers, Arrhenos social files, Banking War) and terse reference (setting/, Arrhenos government/military/economy, most location files). The two-register structure is a feature, not a bug — but a handful of files sit *below* reference grade (stubs), one file is stranded in the old voice inside a rewritten power, and the appendices haven't caught up with the canon the rewrites created.

---

## 1. Bugs and contradictions (small, do first)

1. **The two Long Descents.** The bar in Verenstad's Transient Quarter ([transient-quarter.md:32](worlds/arrhenos/locations/verenstad-gamma/transient-quarter.md), founded 3741 C per the timeline) and the navigation-landmark bar at [Korsen's Anchorage:33](powers/union-freeholds/locations/korsen's-anchorage.md) share a name — almost certainly an accidental collision from the Freeholds rewrite. *Recommended fix: canonize it.* "The Long Descent" as the spacer's default bar name — every port has one, none are related, the origin is disputed in every port that has one. Cheap, charming, anti-homogeneous.
2. **Elysian age math.** The timeline founds the Collective in **3089 C** (773 years ago), but five passages say "six centuries" ([index.md:29](powers/elysian-collective/index.md), [governance.md:59](powers/elysian-collective/governance.md), [culture.md:47](powers/elysian-collective/culture.md), [the-garden.md:26](powers/elysian-collective/locations/the-garden.md) ×2). *Cleanest fix:* 3089 = founding of the loose "Terraforming Collective"; the guild Charter and the Garden date from ~3260 C — "six centuries since the Charter" becomes true, and the gap itself is lore (the commonwealth-to-guild slide the index already describes).
3. **Broken links.** [brovdingonai/index.md:25](worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/index.md) links "The Marketplace" to `marketplace.md`, which doesn't exist — it's [shambles.md](worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/shambles.md). [sol-system.md:20](worlds/sol-system.md) links `../powers/neo-solar-republic.md` — now a directory; should be `neo-solar-republic/index.md`.
4. **Vega population wobble.** Table says ~1.4 trillion ([vega index](powers/vega-throne/index.md), [powers/index.md](powers/index.md)); prose says "one and a half trillion" (vega index :3, [government.md:5](powers/vega-throne/government.md), [media.md:3](powers/vega-throne/media.md)). Pick one (1.4 and "nearly one and a half" both work).
5. **Drift population arithmetic.** ~900 billion across "thousands of havens" ([overview.md](powers/drift-communities/overview.md)) means an *average* haven holds hundreds of millions — but Ascendant, a flagship Grand Haven, holds 4.2M. Either havens number in the *hundreds of thousands* (nice: makes the Drift vaster and less knowable) or the population is tens of billions. Recommend the former — "thousands of havens" becomes "the great havens number in the thousands; the full fleet, nobody has ever counted."
6. **Timeline vs. Cartel canon.** [timeline.md:34](appendices/timeline.md) says "Sable Cartel incorporation, 3203 C" — but [structure.md:61](powers/sable-cartel/structure.md) canonizes that the Cartel *has no founding*, only accretion. Fix the timeline line: "3203 C — traditional (and unverifiable) date of the Sable Cartel's incorporation; the Cartel itself has never confirmed one."
7. **The Empty Quarter Event (3644 C).** Appears exactly once in the entire corpus ([timeline.md:44](appendices/timeline.md)), bolded, unexplained. Decide: cut it, or (better) keep it as a deliberate mystery and give it three sentences somewhere — see §4.7.
8. **Micro-nit:** Mandate "nine centuries deep" / Sapphire Steps "nine hundred years" vs. Modern Mandate founded 2847 C (1,015 years ago). Either "ten centuries" or let the First-Dynasty ambiguity absorb it. Low priority.

## 2. Tweaks (wording-level)

Expand into the canonical chronology: Grey Incursion 3567, Labour Wars 3672–89, Pirate Fleets 3712, Sefkir expulsion 3829, Banking War 3834–39, Kraeven attainder 3840, NSR Census expansions 3840/3850/3860, Cassini Incident 3845, Bureau leak 3854, aurite strike 3858, Yansieve 3858–, gate-efficiency proof 3861. One afternoon; big consistency payoff.
- [navigation-data.md](appendices/navigation-data.md) / [diplomatic-protocols.md](appendices/diplomatic-protocols.md): mostly placeholders. Either give each two or three concrete entries in-voice or fold them into other appendices.

## 3. Rewrites (bring stragglers up to house voice)

1. **[lazarus-project.md](powers/elysian-collective/lazarus-project.md)** — the one Elysian file still in the old flat register ("### The Problem / ### The Opportunity") while its content is some of the best in the power (Tender deaths, Solveig, the Anomalies). Deserves the full treatment; the Anomalies section alone (unrecognisable terraforming signatures on three pre-record worlds) is a major mystery hook currently buried in bullet points.
2. **[nightlife.md](worlds/arrhenos/locations/brovdingonai/nightlife.md)** — an actual stub ("*to be expanded... an extremely seedy strip club*"), and it's the link target for `intimacy.md`'s commercial-intimacy reference. Small file, disproportionate embarrassment.
3. **Optional, low priority:** Arrhenos [government.md](worlds/arrhenos/government.md) / [military.md](worlds/arrhenos/military.md) are skeletal next to the Mandate's equivalents. As reference cards they're serviceable; a half-voice pass (one framing paragraph each, in the vein of "the republic's government is designed to be boring, and considers that its finest achievement") would close the register gap without bloating them.

## 4. Expansions (ranked by leverage)

1. **A minor-powers gazetteer** (`powers/minor-powers.md` or `worlds/independents.md`). The overview promises ~50 major regional powers, ~200 minor, thousands of polities — and the corpus documents none of them. This is also where every doctrine from the homogeneity pass gets its proof: a devout world, a rival regional lingua franca, a polity that refuses the quid, a *second* neutral banking house, a regional media culture, another gender experiment (the "cousinly correspondence" now canonized in women-and-gender needs an address). Eight to twelve entries at a paragraph or two each — the single highest-leverage file the setting could add.
2. **One Mandate world that isn't Tiamming.** Everything Mandate-located is the throne world, which quietly re-shrinks the empire the culture file worked to expand. One provincial-world file — a slow province where the serials arrive years old, worship is recorded as folklore, the prefect *is* the government, and the citizen-subject line is enforced strangely — cashes half the Mandate's own claims at once.
3. **The Drift beyond Ascendant.** The overview insists Ascendant is "emphatically not the template" — then documents nothing else. One **Trade Haven** file (thin margins, the circuit, what a bad season means) and a short **Refugee Haven** file would make the Drift's internal inequality real. The overview already wrote their theses.
4. **Yansieve from inside.** Currently visible only through the blockade, which is thematically perfect and practically limiting. A file built as *contested sources* — a blockade-runner's testimony, a rebel council transcript, a Bureau assessment, none agreeing — keeps the ambiguity the Throne enforces while giving the rebellion faces. The corpus's biggest live story arc deserves one file of its own ground.
5. **A Little Arrhenos.** The diaspora rewrite created the mixed second generation, the enclave bathhouse, the Vega posting as "the making of careers" — one location file (Little Arrhenos in Lyra or a Vega orbital: the bath, the counting-house, the kids who code-switch pronouns, the Ministry liaison with his glacial paperwork) turns the new doctrine into a place.
6. **Tethys-Var.** Load-bearing for the economy (fez), the Cartel (its most contested operation), and the NSR (gate research threatens it) — and currently a thin reference card. Port Lophore, the Tethys Consortium as a character, harvest-season boomtown culture, the illegal harvesters.
7. **The mysteries, curated.** Three dangling hooks — the **Empty Quarter Event** (3644 C, timeline only), **Kepler-442** (classified deployment), and the **Lazarus Anomalies** (unrecognisable terraforming) — are each good precisely because unexplained. Recommendation: don't solve any of them; give each a home paragraph (what is *rumoured*, per the usual contradictory sources) and let them gesture at each other without confirming links. A one-page `appendices/mysteries.md` in the compiled-document voice ("this office declines to speculate") would do it.
8. **Campottonì directory.** The capital of the setting's most-developed world is a 30-line stub. The Moss, the Spires, the First Fathers, and the University of Campottonì (already canon as Haven Ascendant's academic partner — a lovely thread to pull) merit the Brovdingonai treatment.
9. **Vega's agricultural worlds.** The culture rewrite gave them their own serials, stars, and gossip; the locations index promises "worlds beyond the Vega system." One file (harvest world, its own celebrity culture, Australis-adjacent temperament, the court a rumour) completes the audience-is-not-one-audience doctrine.
10. **A Verdant company world.** The Cartel files gesture at valley towns, harvest calendars, and local shrines under a paymaster no one thinks about. One small file from ground level — where "the Cartel" is the name of nothing and Verdant is the name of everything — would be the Cartel's Wen Daoshi.
11. **Landwick and the Nemora.** Landwick has one neighbourhood (the Tep, excellent); "additional neighbourhoods to be documented." The Nemora promises seven cities and documents one. Low urgency, steady returns.
12. **Sol system.** Modest expansion: Earth under the UN (pilgrimage economics, what the oldest government does all day), Mars's parishes and poles. The NSR files are rich enough that Sol's thinness now shows.

## 5. Cross-cutting projects

1. **Name-collision sweep.** Known duplicates, some fine, some accidental: **Long Descent** ×2 (see §1.1); **Covenant** ×4 (Covenant Station, Dustmote's Covenant city, Roughneck's Covenant Basin, Rootbound Covenant); **Meridian/Meridine** ×4 (Vega house, Lyra continent, Haven reconstruction district, Meridian Broadcasting — the Haven's "Meridian District" is the accidental one; consider renaming it, e.g. "the Paulista District"); **Tam** ×3 (Tam Orel, Tam Essien, Oriol Tam); **Vex** ×2 (Vex Calder, Vex Orien). Within-Vega echoes (Aurelian house/continent/city, Radiant Court/Radiant Productions) read as deliberate and can stay. Decide each: canon-with-a-wink, rename, or leave.
2. **A geography of human space.** There is no spatial picture: where the powers sit relative to one another, what "trailing frontier" and "Andromedan frontier" mean, roughly how far Arrhenos–Tiamming–Vega–Sol are from each other (the comm file gives Arrhenos–Tiamming ≈ 180 ly — the only fixed distance in the corpus). A short prose primer in `setting/` (not necessarily a map) would anchor navigation-data and every "weeks from anywhere" claim.
3. **Story-seeds index.** The corpus is now dense with wound springs: three successions (Xuanzong's empty throne, Caspian's empty cradle, Caelum's untested nephew), Meridian-vs-Valdorian, the Sefkir example spreading, the Census's direction of travel, the Tender franchise, Jace Holloway Sr.'s general strike, gate research vs. the fez economy, Dustmote's reversal research, the Ministry's evolving line on diaspora children. A one-page index of live tensions (with file pointers) would make the setting usable at a glance for fiction or play — worth asking what the setting is *for* before building it.
4. **"On sources" appendix.** The corpus consistently uses vantage notes and contested claims; a half-page in-universe note on who compiles these documents (and what they admit they cannot verify) would license every deliberate ambiguity in one place. Optional flavor, high charm-per-word.

## 6. Questions for the author

- **What is the setting for?** (Fiction, RPG, pleasure of the thing?) Decides whether §5.3 matters and how much location grain to build.
- **Appetite for new canon vs. consolidation?** §4.1–5 add canon; §1–3 consolidate. Both are ready to go; the order above assumes fixes-first.
- **The mysteries:** happy to keep Empty Quarter / Kepler-442 / Lazarus Anomalies permanently unexplained (my recommendation), or should one of them secretly connect to something?
